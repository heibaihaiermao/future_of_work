### Updated Work Description    
**Manager, Evaluation and Strategic Planning (EC-07)**  
   
#### **Summary**  
The Manager, Evaluation and Strategic Planning applies advanced automation, AI-enabled analytics, and digital-first processes to lead, coordinate, and deliver the evaluation and strategic planning functions for Statistics Canada's programs, policies, and initiatives. Leading at the intersection of technological innovation and public stewardship, this role shapes the identification, assessment, and communication of program effectiveness, efficiency, and future needs, supporting evidence-driven decision-making and continuous organizational improvement.  
   
---  
   
#### **Key Responsibilities and Activities**  
   
1. **Strategic Evaluation Leadership**  
   - Plans, leads, and coordinates economic and socio-economic analyses and evaluation projects across Statistics Canada, ensuring all processes leverage automated, AI-powered pipelines for data gathering, synthesis, benchmarking, and reporting.  
   - Directs the integration of digital analytics, continuous performance monitoring, automated KPI dashboards, and feedback loops, surfacing real-time insights for management action.  
   - Oversees the design and application of qualitative and quantitative methodologies that exploit automation and AI to maximize efficiency and analytic quality.  
   
2. **Stakeholder Consultation and Needs Assessment**  
   - Orchestrates stakeholder consultations using AI-powered digital engagement and analysis tools to synthesize internal and external needs and feedback, surfacing optimized solution options and draft business cases for review.  
   - Negotiates and secures agreement on project scopes, resource requirements, and courses of action, using system-generated reports and recommendations as the basis for collaborative decision-making.  
   - Confirms and contextualizes needs identified and prioritized via automated systems, focusing personal effort on complex cases requiring nuanced negotiation or advanced interpretation.  
   
3. **Project and Resource Coordination**  
   - Initiates and coordinates cross-functional projects, employing digital collaboration, automated scheduling, and resource estimation tools to ensure efficient delivery of high-impact studies and evaluations.  
   - Assigns tasks and oversees workflows using AI-enabled monitoring platforms, intervening for milestone review, exception management, and iterative improvement.  
   
4. **Continuous Feedback and Improvement**  
   - Leverages AI and digital orchestration to automate the continual monitoring and analysis of program outcomes, user feedback, process metadata, and performance indicators.  
   - Leads the analysis and communication of automated evaluation findings, options, and improvement recommendations, ensuring contextual alignment with strategic objectives before executive action.  
   - Facilitates rapid-cycle improvement, acting on system-generated action plans and tracking the implementation of enhancements through digital dashboards and feedback systems.  
   
5. **Development and Communication of Evaluation Standards**  
   - Defines, maintains, and oversees automated standards, performance indicators, and quality assurance procedures, ensuring integration within organization-wide AI-enabled monitoring platforms.  
   - Provides interpretive oversight and periodically reviews the escalation thresholds and action rules for automated exceptions in evaluation and reporting workflows.  
   
6. **Strategic Planning and Innovation**  
   - Drives the modernization and digital transformation of evaluation and planning practices, championing the adoption and scaling of automation, AI analytics, and process digitization across the evaluation function.  
   - Develops and recommends strategies for integrating program evaluation methodologies and innovative monitoring tools into the design and operation of new and ongoing programs, policies, and initiatives.  
   
7. **Reporting, Briefing, and Presentation**  
   - Oversees the drafting of evaluation and planning reports, briefings, presentations, and recommendations using AI-generated content as a starting point, focusing manual input on interpretive value, nuanced findings, and senior client communications.  
   - Ensures documentation, presentations, and dashboards are standards-based, machine-actionable, and ready for digital dissemination to stakeholders and executives.  
   
8. **Consultative and Advisory Services**  
   - Provides expert advisory services to departmental clients and senior executives using outputs from automated systems, supporting strategic, annual, and long-term evaluation plans, policy development, and resource planning.  
   - Trains and mentors staff and client groups on leveraging digital tools, interpreting AI-generated analytics, and utilizing rapid-cycle improvement practices.  
   
---  
   
#### **Required Knowledge and Competencies**  
   
- **Digital Leadership:** Ability to oversee digital, automated, and AI-supported evaluation, planning, and reporting pipelines.  
- **AI/Automation Fluency:** Skilled in specifying and validating automated data gathering, feedback synthesis, benchmarking routines, and digital reporting workflows.  
- **Change Management:** Strong capability to lead modernization and digital transformation initiatives, ensuring organizational buy-in and successful uptake of new analytic and evaluation platforms.  
- **Advanced Consultation:** Proficient in interpreting, negotiating, and prioritizing system-surfaced needs and options; adept at stakeholder engagement in digital environments.  
- **Analytical Excellence:** Expertise in interpreting automated analytic outputs, dashboards, and recommendations; resolving exceptions and making judgments in ambiguous, novel, or high-stakes contexts.  
- **Communication:** Skilled in contextualizing and presenting AI-generated content for executive and stakeholder audiences, ensuring clarity, actionable insight, and alignment with strategic objectives.  
- **Continuous Improvement:** Commitment to data-driven performance measurement, digital iterative improvement cycles, and embedding “quality by design” in all evaluation processes.  
   
---  
   
#### **Impact of Digital Modernization**  
In the modern statistical organization, the Manager, Evaluation and Strategic Planning (EC-07) is pivotal in bridging advanced analytics, digital transformation, and public sector stewardship. The role’s success hinges on maximizing efficiency, responsiveness, and quality through automation and AI support—allowing human effort to focus on high-value problem solving, strategic decision-making, and the stewardship of complex or nuanced activities that technology cannot automate.
