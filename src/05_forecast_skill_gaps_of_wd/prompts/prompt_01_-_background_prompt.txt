## Background
Updates were extracted from a powerpoint presentation on the future of work at our organization. Then those updates were applied to the GSBPM, a high-level standard document defining the activities at National Statistical Organizations. Using the update elements (including original description, implicated updates, justification for implication, reasoning regarding application of updates, and updated work description), work-descriptions were flagged as "implicated" in appropriate update elements. These "implicated" updates were collected for each work-description, reasoning was layered-in, and a proposal was made for updating each work-description. One work description will be provided at the end.
