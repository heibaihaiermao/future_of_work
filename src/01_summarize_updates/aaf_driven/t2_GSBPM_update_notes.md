## Phase 1 Summary

The application of updates to sub-processes in the phase demonstrated common
elements, but with variable quality. In particular, updates from slide 4

"
   - End-to-End Process Automation with Minimal Manual Intervention;

   - Automated, AI-powered data pipelines- from acquisition to dissemination-
     reduce manual effort, increase speed, quality, and enable real-time
     responsiveness across all stages of the statistical process.

   - Employee input focuses on higher value work, innovation, and
     interpretation, not execution.
"

is applied to phase 1 and all sub-processes except for 1.5, and largely
boils down to "let AI do it, human SME validates".

An update from slide 6:

    "Clear delineation sponsor (subject matter) roles and accountabilities,
    based on the minimum requirement that sponsors / subject matter are
    responsible for specification and certification (1.4, 1.5, 3.1, 4.4)"

Is applied to 1.4, and 1.5. There is only one other update
(slide 6: creation of centre of expertise) that is applied (to sub-process
1.5)


My first impression is concern that the updates to the GSBPM will boil down
to "SME do everything, AI does all of the work." Such an augmentation is
not incorrect, but it may degrade specificity in the descriptions.

The worst example of this is from sub-process 1.6. There, the context of
"conform[ity] to the requirements of the approval body", listing elements
of a business case, shifts to (in the updated description)

    "The business case is reviewed and approved by the relevant body, with
    human expertise providing the final evaluation and decision."

The elements of the business case are lost, as is a explict call for a
"go/no-go" decision.


A loss of specificity may be acceptable, or even valuable, for the
purpose of updating work-descriptions.

This depends on the degree to which the specifics of the GSBPM phases
are reflected in the work-descriptions.

If the specific processes of the GSBPM are only *indirectly* related to
the elements of a work-description, then a loss of specificity but
an emphasis on new or existing elements of work-descriptions would be
fit for purpose.
