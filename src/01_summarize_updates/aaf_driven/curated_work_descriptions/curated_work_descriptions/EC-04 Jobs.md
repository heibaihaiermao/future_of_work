<th><strong>EC-04</strong></th>
<td><strong>Evaluation Analyst</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Participates as a member of project teams, in the conduct of
evaluation studies related to Statistics Canada programs, policies and
initiatives, to evaluate their relevance and performance, under the
supervision of the Evaluation Manager.</p></li>
<li><p>Makes recommendations to the Evaluation Manager on the nature and
scope of evaluations, evaluation approaches, and makes recommendations
on general data / information requirements, indicators, as well as
modifications to analytical methods and techniques used in
studies.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops and / or carries out specific elements of
Statistics Canada evaluation projects such as, conducting literature and
data research; identifying, gathering, analyzing and synthesizing
quantitative and qualitative data; participating in the conduct of
interviews and focus groups; applying analytical tools and techniques to
determine implications of evaluation results and formulating
conclusions.</p></li>
<li><p>Researches, identifies and selects information for evaluations
from a variety of sources; analyzes information related to
Statistics Canada business lines, policy, program performance, program
relevance, success and cost-effectiveness.</p></li>
<li><p>Assesses current and future requirements for statistical data;
interprets and analyzes statistical information and databases to support
findings and conclusions concerning various elements of
Statistics Canada evaluations.</p></li>
<li><p>Drafts and writes reports, presentations, briefing notes and
memoranda for senior evaluation staff and management on elements of
program evaluation related to the various Statistics Canada
programs.</p></li>
<li><p>Attends departmental and inter-departmental meetings, when
required, to discuss files, issues and evaluation requirements;
interprets and reports on trends; and analyzes the effects of new or
revised policies concerning program evaluation.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication:</strong></p>
<p>Written skills are required to synthesize, clarify and interpret
complex, technical and sensitive information relating to
Statistics Canada program evaluations, and to adapt this information
into formats that can be understood by audiences who may have a limited
knowledge of the subject matter. Written skills are required to draft
reports, briefing notes and presentations dealing with complex and
politically sensitive issues. This involves the requirement to explain,
synthesize, clarify and interpret technical issues related to
Statistics Canada program evaluation and to ensure that the information
is understood. These documents are used by senior colleagues, senior
management and departmental officials, taking into consideration that
departmental officials may have limited knowledge of the subject
matter. </p>
<p>Oral presentation skills are required to provide briefings and advice
concerning evaluation issues, processes and methodologies, to managers
and colleagues. Liaison and consultation skills are required to maintain
client / stakeholder networks and foster collaborative working
relationships with clients and stakeholders, as well as, to conduct
interviews with managers and staff. Communication skills are required to
effectively interact with other government officials, members of
interest groups and the public, who may have limited knowledge of the
subject matter.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of Specialized Fields:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of theories and principles of economics, socio-economic,
demography and statistics to: participate in the conduct of evaluation
studies relating to Statistics Canada programs, policies and
initiatives; be a member of project teams and interpret and analyze
statistical information and databases related to various elements of
Statistics Canada evaluations conducted.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories, methods and techniques of research, including
social science research and analysis in order to: undertake literature
and data research in support of Statistics Canada program evaluation
projects; identify, synthesize and analyze qualitative and quantitative
research data; conduct interviews and participate in focus groups.</p>
<p>Knowledge of program evaluation and performance measurement theories,
processes and practices to participate in the conduct of evaluation
studies, to make recommendations concerning the nature and scope of
studies and study approaches, and to draft and write reports, memoranda
and briefing notes based on elements of studies conducted.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual Knowledge:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge is required of Statistics Canada’s structure, organization,
mandate, vision, business lines, program structure, organizational
hierarchy, departmental policies and guidelines concerning evaluation of
the relevance and performance of programs; the federal role in
statistical information; and departmental stakeholders. This knowledge
is required to develop and carry out specific elements of evaluation
studies.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of the roles, responsibilities of central
agencies, as well as underlying legislation of Statistics Canada
programs, to enable the incumbent to participate in the horizontal
analysis of programs, policies and initiatives.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of emerging trends and developments in
provincial / territorial data systems to enable the incumbent to
participate in the analysis of the work of Statistics Canada, and their
programs in the broader social, economic and political context.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of emerging trends and developments in the
Canadian private sector, academia and NGOs in order to facilitate
appropriate analysis and identify evaluation implications with respect
to the mandate of Statistics Canada.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of departmental legislation of
Statistics Canada, central agencies, Parliament accountability and
reporting requirements, government and departmental regulations,
policies and legislation as they apply to evaluation program activities,
issues, processes and methods, such as the Public Service Values and
Ethics Code, the <em>Policy on Results, Privacy and Access to
Information Acts</em>. This knowledge is required to participate in
Statistics Canada program evaluations and studies.</p>
<p><strong>Research and Analysis:</strong></p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to select, identify and utilize
information for studies, from a variety of sources, including
interviews, Statistics Canada documents and those of other government
departments, information from provincial and territorial governments, as
well as internet sources of information. The incumbent must also analyze
and synthesize quantitative and qualitative research data, and to apply
analytical tools and techniques in order to determine the impact of
evaluation studies.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to conduct assessments of current and
future requirements for statistical data; interpreting and analyzing
statistical information / databases to support findings and conclusions
concerning various elements of Statistics Canada evaluations
conducted.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to make recommendations to the
Evaluation Manager on the extent, nature, scope, objectives and
approaches to evaluation studies, modifications to analytical methods
and techniques to be used in studies and success indicators. The
incumbent is required to consider the impact of variables on studies,
including changes to programs and policies.</p></td>
</tr>

<th><strong>EC-04</strong></th>
<td><strong>Metadata Development, Dissemination and Evaluation
Coordinator</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Plans, prioritizes, coordinates and undertakes the development,
preparation, editing, dissemination and validation of statistical
metadata, ensuring that it is correctly loaded to the corporate metadata
repository.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides assistance, advice and direction to internal clients
with respect to statistical metadata, documented standards and relevant
policies, directives and guidelines, negotiating project plans,
priorities, procedural modifications and target dates.</p></li>
<li><p>Assesses the need to modify current or proposed methods and
procedures for producing, storing, disseminating and evaluating
metadata, assists in the development of recommended improvements and
develops specifications for the testing of new procedures and
systems.</p></li>
<li><p>Recommends, develops, writes, edits and approves documentation,
procedures manuals and training manuals related to the production,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata, organizes and
gives presentations and coordinates and delivers training.</p></li>
<li><p>Assists in the development of procedures and tools for evaluating
the quality of statistical metadata, undertakes metadata quality
evaluations and compiles and writes reports for clients and
management.</p></li>
<li><p>Leads project teams and supervises work units tasked with the
development, production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of
statistical metadata and participates as a statistical metadata
representative in working groups and on development projects.</p></li>
<li></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Provides advice and training with respect to the policies,
directives, guidelines, methods, procedures and systems used in the
production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata to subordinates, other statistical metadata specialists,
clients and managers who use the information as input to on-going
operations and to decisions concerning the management of statistical
metadata at Statistics Canada.</p>
<p>Liaises with other statistical metadata specialists, subject matter
specialists, survey managers and management in order to develop and
translate requirements into specifications for the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata; to establish
project plans, priorities and target dates; to negotiate modifications
to methods, procedures and timelines; to provide training, and to
suggest improvements to methods, procedures and systems.</p>
<p>Writes and presents detailed documents, procedures manuals and
training manuals which explain, clarify and make recommendations with
respect to the methods, procedures and systems used to produce, store
and disseminate statistical metadata and which are used by other
statistical metadata specialists, systems specialists, clients, and
management in the operation and enhancement of statistical metadata
management programs.</p>
<p>Writes reports which describe policy requirements related to the
development, production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of high
quality metadata, which describe the results of metadata quality
evaluations and which make recommendations for procedures and tools that
could be developed to evaluate the quality of statistical
metadata. These reports are used by clients and management to ensure the
quality and consistency of statistical metadata disseminated by
Statistics Canada.</p>
<p><strong>4. Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of statistical metadata concepts and frameworks used within
Statistics Canada, the Government of Canada and internationally, and
knowledge of how those concepts and frameworks are used and reflected in
policies, methods and procedures relating to the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata in order to plan,
prioritize, coordinate and undertake the preparation, editing,
validation and quality evaluation of statistical metadata, to ensure
that colleagues and clients understand the importance of adhering to
policies, methods and procedures in the preparation, storage and
dissemination of metadata and to assess the need for modifications to
methods and procedures.</p>
<p>Knowledge of methods, procedures and systems used for producing,
storing, disseminating and evaluating the quality of statistical
metadata and the potential impacts of new or modified methods,
procedures and systems on workloads, outputs and clients in order to
establish project plans, priorities, timelines and training plans; to
recommend improvements to methods, procedures and training; to
participate in the development of procedures and tools for evaluating
the quality of statistical metadata and to explain the results of
metadata quality evaluations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of survey and statistical program subject matter concepts,
methods and techniques relevant to the collection, processing,
certification and dissemination of data in order to evaluate client
requirements for metadata production, storage and dissemination; to
implement plans for the production, storage, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata; and to more accurately and
efficiently assess the quality of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of management techniques and principles to manage financial
and human resources, either directly or within a matrix management
environment.</p>
<p>Knowledge of software, statistical packages and programming languages
used in the production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata.</p>
<p><strong>5. Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of policies, directives and guidelines directly and
specifically related to the production, storage, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata in order to plan and prioritize the
preparation, editing and validation of statistical metadata; to provide
assistance, advice, direction, project plans and training to clients; to
assist in the development of procedures and tools for evaluating
metadata quality; and to effectively undertake evaluations of the
quality of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of contacts within key client Divisions in order to
establish a liaison, to evaluate and understand program statistical
metadata requirements, provide assistance, advice and direction with
respect to policy requirements, methods, procedures, systems, training,
priorities and timelines relevant to the production, storage,
dissemination and quality evaluation of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of surveys and statistical programs within
Statistics Canada in order to locate, analyze and compare related
statistical metadata and to identify, evaluate and ensure consistency in
the methods of processing, storing, disseminating and evaluating the
quality of metadata across programs, identifying and evaluating any
conflicts, gaps or duplication of effort.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and of Statistics Canada policies,
directives and guidelines in order to ensure understanding and
application of the Act and these policies, directives and guidelines in
the production, storage, dissemination and quality evaluation of
statistical metadata.</p>
<p>The position must have an awareness of the Statistics and Privacy
Acts with respect to the security and confidentiality of statistical
information to ensure that actions are in compliance.</p>
<p><strong>6. Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Analyses client requirements in order to determine the feasibility,
procedural options, priorities, and target dates relevant to the
production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata, ensuring that approaches and results are consistent within and
across surveys and statistical programs.</p>
<p>Provides assistance, advice and direction to internal clients with
respect to statistical metadata, documented standards and relevant
policies, directives and guidelines, negotiating procedural
modifications and establishing target dates, project plans and
priorities.</p>
<p>Analyzes current production, storage, dissemination and evaluation
methods, procedures and systems to ascertain their relevancy and to
determine the need for changes or alternative methods, procedures and
systems which would result in operational efficiencies and/or
improvements in the quality of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Analyzes changes in methods, procedures and systems used to produce,
store, disseminate and evaluate the quality of statistical metadata in
order to determine the need to update statistical metadata or adapt it
to new formats; to determine where updates are required to existing
documentation, procedures manuals and training manuals; and to determine
potential short-term and long-term impacts on clients.</p>
<p>Analyzes and evaluates statistical metadata with respect to policy
and procedural requirements and consistency across survey and
statistical programs. Evaluates current and proposed methods, procedures
and tools for evaluating the quality of statistical metadata in order to
suggest potential improvements.</p></td>
</tr>

<th><strong>EC-03</strong></th>
<td><strong>Data Analyst Scientist</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Conducts studies and/or projects using quantitative statistical
data analysis methods, such as: data wrangling, validation, mining, and
visualization (in R/RStudio, Python, SQL, Java, JavaScript or another
software), as well as software development, application of machine/deep
learning and artificial intelligence techniques for complex problem
solving.</p></li>
<li><p>Conducts the acquisition, analysis and integration of large
statistical data sets from a wide variety of structured and unstructured
data sources to identify patterns and trends contributing to the
development of solutions to complex business problems.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in the development of statistical measures and data
analysis techniques and processes, survey development, survey
methodology, or components of data governance (e.g., policies, processes
and standards, privacy, data quality, risk management) and on the use of
data to optimize business value.</p></li>
<li><p>Assesses the social, economic, implications of data to ensure
that the development of policies, strategies, programs and other
initiatives take into consideration the business foresight revealed from
data analytics.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides ongoing tracking, monitoring and reporting of the
performance of decision systems and accuracy of statistical
models.</p></li>
<li><p>Prepares a variety of knowledge products to present empirical
findings of data analytics projects such as reports, dashboards,
presentations, speaking points, technical documents or briefing material
to guide and support departmental and government direction in data
strategy and information requirements.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in multi-disciplinary project teams in the
development of analysis of economic, social and/or statistical analysis
and data analytics and provides advice and recommendations on study
results and/or conclusions.</p></li>
<li><p>Maintains and enhances client/stakeholder networks in matters
relating to data science and analytics, to optimize business value of
data, to determine the implications on policy and programs and the
decision-making process.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p>Judgement is required to track, identify and analyse data from
diverse sources, the effects of data analyses on projects and
initiatives and to outline implications, for review by senior analysts
and/or managers.</p>
<p>Judgement is required when recommending data/information
requirements, approaches and analytical methods for data analysis
studies.</p>
<p>Judgement is also required when making recommendations concerning the
design, development and sharing of data analysis and knowledge products
internally and externally.</p>
<p>The work contributes to the effective management of policy
files/issues; assists in the preparation of discussion, policy and
position papers, briefing notes and presentations. The work also may
lead to improved analytical or statistical methodologies and
processes.</p>
<p>Judgement to discuss business information without divulging
confidential information.</p>
<p>Participates on project teams and working groups. Provides assistance
and guidance to new employees as required.</p>
<p>Assists in planning and organizing work, organizes and consolidates
information from a variety of sources to establish working files and to
develop and/or maintain databases.</p>
<p>May perform administrative tasks to support the unit in the conduct
of studies, the organization and coordination of meetings and other unit
activities.</p>
<p>Personal use and care of a computer and related software; standard
office equipment, furnishings and supplies; reference material and
policy documents; and multimedia equipment. May handle sensitive
materials.</p>
<p>Uses taxi chits and travel credit cards for authorized business
purposes.</p>
<p>Determine the Division’s data requirements and researches and
collects data from a variety of sources, aggregating it so that it
provides valid and reliable data on which to perform quantitative,
retrospective and predictive analysis that measures the impact of the
Department’s programs, both at the national level and regional levels to
support evidence-based policy and program decisions.</p>
<p>Apply advanced data science principles including data capture and
acquisition, data extraction, warehousing, cleansing, modeling,
summarization, reporting, visualization, and exploratory/confirmatory,
predictive analysis, regression, and qualitative analysis, to understand
the implications of analyses for the department’s policies and programs
and formulate conclusions and recommendations.</p>
<p>Review data collections and information sources to determine the
usefulness of existing sources, to propose changes to the data
collection approach and to assess the feasibility and impact of new or
modified methodologies, to identify and evaluate problems in the
processing, editing, compilation and presentation of analytical
materials, to provide solutions or recommendations to management, and to
resolve subject-matter, conceptual and methodological problems.</p>
<p>Plan research and data analytics projects in the design, development
and implementation of data analytic techniques, including artificial
intelligence models and data visualization. Develop/prototype potential
uses of these techniques to enhance STATCAN’s data assets and advance
the evolution of analytics, reporting processes and data governance
capabilities that generate more meaningful data in support of both
operational and strategic, and program and policy decision making.</p>
<p>Create and test data and mathematical models for bias, of particular
important in predictive and prescriptive analytics.</p>
<p>Drafts technical, position and policy papers, briefing notes, and
presentations, which provide critical input regarding the economic and
Statistics implications of data science studies. This information is
also used for planning and decision making for an array of key policies,
programs, legislation and regulations by the Department and
stakeholders.</p></td>
</tr>

<th><strong>EC-04</strong></th>
<td><strong>Operations Development Officer</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Develops and coordinates the implementation, conduct and
maintenance of manual and automated operations for the collection,
capture, coding and processing of data.</p></li>
<li><p>Reviews data inconsistency and integrity problems, recommends new
approaches and resolves reporting and respondent relations
issues.</p></li>
<li><p>Evaluates changes in statistical needs of clients, as well as
trends and developments in data collection, capture, coding and
processing.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides advisory and consultative services to users regarding
collection and production efficiency and/or quality.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates as a statistical operations representative on teams
and working groups throughout the department.</p></li>
<li><p>Leads project teams involved in the collection, capture, coding
and processing of statistical data.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Provides information and advice to managers, employees and the
general public on the development, operation and use of divisional
systems and processes and the use of program results.</p>
<p>Discusses requirements with subject matter and systems specialists,
clients, service providers and others to establish and elaborate the
framework and conditions within which statistical operations will occur.
There is a requirement to understand the issues and resolve differences
that can affect the efficiency of the division's operations and quality
of outputs.</p>
<p>Writes and presents subject matter and technical concepts,
definitions and terms in common terminology in order to explain and
clarify operational systems and processes and/or describe program
results for data suppliers and users who may not be familiar with the
subject matter or systems processes.</p>
<p>Prepares and explains specifications and guidelines to be used by
others in developing and operating systems and processes.</p>
<p>Participates in inter-departmental meetings, conferences and working
groups in order to share knowledge within the area of operational
expertise.</p>
<p><strong>4. Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of current or emerging economic and/or social issues
relating to the program area, in order to identify and resolve data
collection and production problems as well as quality issues.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the concepts, methods and techniques used in the
collection, capture, coding and processing of statistical data in order
to manage, control and improve survey collection, capture, coding and
processing activities.</p>
<p>Knowledge of system analysis and design, computer processing,
generalized survey software packages to manage the design, development,
testing, maintenance and implementation of processing systems; to design
specifications for the testing of data, and to maintain and use
databases.</p>
<p>Knowledge of high level programming language, utility programs and/or
statistical packages in order to retrieve and manipulate data.</p>
<p>Knowledge of project and time management methods, practices and
techniques to develop work plans, estimate resource requirements and to
monitor and report on work progress.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the principles and practices of human and financial
resource management is required to lead project teams.</p>
<p><strong>5. Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of divisional and departmental mandate, objectives,
priorities, practices, standards, processes and procedures to order to
coordinate and manage on-going operational activities and/or special
projects.</p>
<p>Knowledge of divisional operating systems and requirements in order
implement and maintain operational systems and applications.</p>
<p>Knowledge of departmental publication, marketing and dissemination
policies and practices in order to establish common divisional policies
and practices in the preparation of statistical tables, charts,
explanatory notes etc. for dissemination to user and/or subject matter
clients.</p>
<p>Knowledge of statistical programs within Statistics Canada related to
the area of expertise in order to identify potential sources of
information for development and operations and/or special studies and to
identify and evaluate possible data conflicts.</p>
<p>Knowledge of contacts within key client departments to establish a
liaison, to understand and interpret program and user need for
statistical data, and to provide advice and guidance on program
concepts, methods, techniques and results.</p>
<p>Knowledge of collection/respondent relations as it pertains to the
specific program area.</p>
<p>Knowledge of products and services provided by suppliers/vendors is
required to evaluate and recommend the purchase of new equipment and/or
software.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and other pertinent acts such as the
Corporate Returns Act and Income Tax Act, and of Statistics Canada
policies to explain legislative and policy requirements to data
suppliers, users and others with respect to the collection, capture,
coding and processing of data. Knowledge of the Statitstics and Privacy
Acts is particulary important with respect to the security and
confidentiality of information and data  provided and to ensure that
they and their subordinates do not release any information which may
identify an individual or group without prior consent.</p>
<p><strong>6. Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Reviews the sources of information, statistical techniques, methods
