        #### <span id="bookmark2">‌</span>Why Now?

        A Pivotal Moment for Statistics Canada

          

        <img
        src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_004.png"
        width="512" height="305" alt="image" />

        Internal Catalysts

        -   New Chief Statistician <span class="p">– A change in
            leadership brings an opportunity to reimagine our future and
            reassert our value.</span>

        -   Refocus on our core business <span class="p">– We are
            zeroing in on what we do best: producing high-quality,
            trusted, and impactful data</span>

        -   Aging systems and declining response rates <span class="p">–
            Foundational elements of our work are under strain and need
            renewal.</span>

        <img
        src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_005.png"
        width="585" height="305" alt="image" />

        External Pressures:

        -   A faster, more complex world <span class="p">– The pace of
            change is accelerating, and the questions we’re being asked
            are more urgent and nuanced than ever.</span>

        -   Eroding trust in institutions <span class="p">– Public
            confidence is increasingly fragile, amplified by
            misinformation and disinformation.</span>

        -   Rising demand for timely, relevant insights
            <span class="p">– Canadians and decision-makers expect
            faster, clearer answers—with no compromise on
            quality.</span>

        -   Shrinking government budgets <span class="p">– We’re
            expected to</span>

        deliver more, and better, with fewer resources

        We are at a critical inflection point—where the world is
        changing, and so must we.

          

        <span style=" color: black; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; text-decoration: none;">
        </span>

        Why Now?

        Because if we want to keep mattering tomorrow, we must redefine
        how we deliver value today. This is our moment to

        <img
        src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_006.png"
        width="1280" height="137" alt="image" />

        choose bold change—to leverage the transformational efforts
        already underway and build a vision of the future that keeps
        Statistics Canada essential, relevant, and resilient.

        <span id="bookmark3">‌</span>Our Direction:

        From managing process to maximizing impact

        We’ve shown that Statistics Canada delivers— especially when it
        matters most. Now, we’re taking the next step: building on that
        legacy to become a more agile, efficient, and responsive
        organization— able to move at speed, adapt to change, and
        deliver sustainable value in a complex and evolving world.

        This isn’t just about new tools—it’s about shifting our focus
        from managing process to maximizing impact. Empowering our
        experts. Deepening our insights. Strengthening our partnerships.

        The Tier 2 committee visions presented today reflect this
        shift—and they sit at the heart of our broader transformation
        model. They help bring to life our shared objective:

        Adapt our statistical programs to the evolving needs and
        aspirations of Canadians.

        <img
        src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_007.png"

        <span id="bookmark4">‌</span>Key priorities of the future state

        <img
        src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_008.png"
        width="92" height="88" alt="image" />

        01

        End-to-End Process Automation with Minimal Manual Intervention

        -   Automated, AI-powered data pipelines—from acquisition to
            dissemination— reduce manual effort, increase speed and
            quality, and enable real-time responsiveness across all
            stages of the statistical business process.

        -   Employee input focuses on higher value work, innovation, and
            interpretation, not execution.

            <img
            src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_009.png"
            width="92" height="87" alt="image" />

            02

            Surveys as a last resort, not first response

            analysis.

        -   Exploiting existing data holdings is the first response for
            new/existing data needs. Admin data and alternative data is
            the second response for new/existing data needs. Surveys are
            only used if necessary and are optimized dynamically based
            on cost-benefit

        -   Designing, rendering and fielding a questionnaire is done
            using AI and takes no more than X time

            <img
            src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_010.png"
            width="92" height="88" alt="image" />

            03

            Searchable, Usable, Actionable Data Assets

        -   All data and metadata are standardized, richly described,
            and machine-actionable—built for interoperability and reuse
            across tools,

            systems, and organizations.

        -   Discovery, understanding, and use are seamless for all types
            of users—whether human or machine—through APIs, metadata
            tagging, and open formats.

        -   Our data ecosystem empowers internal and external users to
            find, consume, and transform data into new insights,
            products, and services.

            <img
            src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_011.png"
            width="92" height="88" alt="image" />

            04

            Modular, Reusable Architecture and Tools & services

        -   The organization has modular tools and microservices to
            increase agility, maintainability, and reuse across
            programs.

        -   Business areas are empowered to prototype and develop smart
            solutions using supported self-service environments, with
            guardrails and shared libraries.

        -   Open-source tools are the default, and employees openly
            share their code.

            <img
            src="1.%20A%20glimpse%20of%20the%20future%20-%20Tier%202%20Committee%20Visions%20for%20the%20future%20of%20the%20agency%20%282%29%20-%20Copy_files/Image_012.png"
            width="1280" height="182" alt="image" />

            05

            A Modern, Insight-Driven Workforce

            -   Analysts and researchers focus on value-add insight, not
                operational tasks—spending more time understanding
                context, building

                relationships, and generating actionable analysis.

            -   Teams are curious, agile, and versatile—fluent in
                open-source tools, data science methods, and
                collaborative problem-solving across disciplines.

            -   Analysts work in multidisciplinary teams that integrate
                design, technology, and subject matter expertise to
                deliver user-centered solutions.

            -   Decisions are made by clearly designated owners—not by
                group consensus—but they are communicated and understood
                by those affected

            ### <span id="bookmark5">‌</span>Next steps

-   What you’ve seen today is more than a collection of committee
    visions—it’s a shared picture of the organization we want to become.
    A faster, more flexible, and more connected Statistics Canada—one
    that leads with insight, empowers its people, and meets the needs of
    a changing world. This is a moment to build on the momentum we
    already have, and to commit to delivering—not just planning—for the
    future.

      

-   To ensure we deliver on the vision presented today:

<!-- -->

-   The Business Planning team will return to EMC with a focused
    execution proposal <span class="s22">to address execution gaps. Once
    approved, the recommendation is to tie the execution to executive
    PMAs.</span>

-   The Transformation Unit will link structure, processes, data and
    technology with the

    organization’s **strategic goals and objectives**.

-   The **visions presented today will be shared with all executives**
    during the Strategic Alignment

Session to ensure broad engagement.

<span id="bookmark10">‌</span>Business Vision

GSBPM Step: Dissemination

Business Vision:

Transform our data dissemination approach by becoming a modern,
digital-first statistical agency. Prioritize automation, metadata
enrichment, and open, machine-readable data structures to ensure our
information is accessible, discoverable, and actionable for all user
groups—including both sophisticated researchers and new AI-powered query
tools. Our goal is to empower users to get the data they need by
themselves, while making our services more efficient, accessible and
visible for those who require a more customized approach.

Key changes required to reach the vision:

Expanded Digital Dissemination<span class="s30">: Shift focus from
static web publishing to dynamic, interactive, and API-driven platforms
for more accessible and real-time data engagement.</span>

Automation of Core Processes<span class="s30">: Automate production and
dissemination of data products (including data tables and PUMFs),
improving speed, accuracy, and resource efficiency.</span>

Metadata-First Approach<span class="s30">: Implement comprehensive
metadata standards and tagging, enabling external tools like ChatGPT,
Gemini, and other AI agents to accurately discover and extract relevant
data.</span>

Clear User Segmentation<span class="s30">: Redesign websites as
specialized platforms primarily targeting advanced users, like
researchers and policymakers, with the understanding that the general
public will increasingly access our data through third-party AI
interfaces. Having options for users that need more support.</span>

Mandatory Open File Formats<span class="s30">: Standardize output
formats (such as SDMX) to ensure compatibility, interoperability, and
ease of use for downstream consumption and integration.</span>

Streamline Publication Workflows<span class="s30">: Streamline and
automate the end-to-end publication process to reduce friction,
duplication, improve consistency, and better support rapid content
updates.</span>

Reevaluate Data Visualization Investments<span class="s30">: Focus less
on proprietary visualizations for public users, recognizing that</span>

users will generate tailored outputs via AI tools.
