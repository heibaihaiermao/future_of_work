<th>EC-06</th>
<td>Senior Evaluation Analyst</td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Coordinates and manages the activities of multi-disciplinary
project teams, combining Statistics Canada and external resources, to
evaluate the relevance and performance of programs, policies and
initiatives, and to provide advice to the Evaluation Manager, the
Director, the Chief Audit and Evaluation Executive, and senior
management.</p></li>
<li><p>Consults with the Evaluation Manager and Statistics Canada
program managers, in order to discuss and recommend project objectives,
scope, approaches to be utilized, the timing and resources required for
studies and projects and negotiates and secures agreement on courses of
action and activities to be taken.</p></li>
<li><p>Initiates and coordinates complex studies and projects involving
own and other government departments, partners and stakeholders;
identifies project activities and study techniques to be applied;
estimates human and financial resource requirements, and makes
recommendations to the Evaluation Manager; schedules and assigns
activities; coordinates and monitors work to ensure project objectives
are met; and manages the design and adaptation of data collection tools
for studies.</p></li>
<li><p>Plans, manages and coordinates economic and socio-economic
analysis of Statistics Canada programs to evaluate their effectiveness
against organizational strategic objectives and priorities. Manages the
selection of methodologies for evaluation studies such as cost-benefit
analysis, and manages the application of analytical tools, including
qualitative and quantitative analysis of data.</p></li>
<li><p>Leads research to assess current and future requirements for
statistical data; conducts analyses of conclusions on a broad and
complex range of studies, and provides advice and recommendations to
senior managers and subject-matter experts concerning changes or
improvements to Statistics Canada policies, strategic plans, programs,
and services as well as redesigns of programs based on studies
conducted.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops evaluation reports, briefings and presentations
outlining results of evaluation studies; develops options, strategies
and recommendations, and provides advice and recommendations to the
Evaluation Manager, Director, Chief Audit and Evaluation Executive and
senior management to improve the effectiveness of policies, programs and
services.</p></li>
<li><p>Consults with representatives of OGDs, other levels of
government, partners and stakeholders on studies and evaluation projects
to be undertaken jointly (e.g., government-wide evaluations). Represents
Statistics Canada and the Evaluation Division at meetings, on committees
and working groups to discuss and share best practices, tools and
approaches.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input and data related to the development of the
Statistics Canada Evaluation Plan, the Departmental Plan, Business Plan,
Departmental Results, and other relevant departmental
documents.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication:</strong></p>
<p>Presentation and negotiation skills are required to secure the
cooperation and buy-in of Statistics Canada managers on the
establishment of the objectives, approaches, schedules, timing and
resource requirements related to program evaluations and studies.</p>
<p>Oral and written skills are required to present and explain to all
levels of management (e.g., manager through to Director General as well
as senior management) conclusions from evaluations and other studies to
gain acceptance of recommendations concerning changes or improvements to
agency policies, plans, programs, and services as well as the redesign
of programs based on studies conducted.</p>
<p>Oral presentation skills are required to explain to Statistics Canada
program managers and staff, or project team members, issues related to
evaluation processes, practices, plans and methodologies, result-based
measurement, management, processes and practices, to obtain cooperation
throughout the project phases.</p>
<p>Negotiation and consultation skills are needed when communicating
with OGDs, partners and stakeholders to establish project / study
requirements and parameters.</p>
<p>Consultation and liaison skills are required to establish and
maintain a variety of networks within and outside of the Federal
government to participate on departmental and interdepartmental
committees, seminars, workshops and task forces with individuals from a
variety of disciplines and knowledge levels. This involves discussing
and resolving issues, working partnerships, gaining and maintaining the
acceptance of members, and developing strong working relationships.</p>
<p>Communication skills are required to consult with officials from
central agencies to exchange and negotiate the type of information
required for program evaluation, to resolve problems, and present
Statistics Canada’s viewpoint concerning service-wide program evaluation
issues.</p>
<p>Writing skills are required to develop and prepare evaluation
reports, papers, briefing materials, presentations, and correspondence.
Oral and written skills are required to negotiate and manage contracts
with external consultants.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of Specialized Fields:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of theories, principles, methodologies, standards,
practices and tools associated with program evaluation, risk management
and performance measurement. These are required to: plan, design and
select the appropriate approaches and methodologies, definitional
devices (logic models, comparative analyses, profiles and case studies),
as well as data collection instruments (focus sessions, interviews,
surveys) to conduct specific evaluations; determine relative strengths
and weaknesses of the approaches; to plan, coordinate and manage program
evaluation projects and studies; manage the application of techniques by
project team members.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories and principles of economics, sociology, social
sciences, demography and statistics to: plan, direct and coordinate
economic and socio-economic analysis of programs to evaluate their
effectiveness against organizational strategic objectives; formulate and
provide recommendations on agency policies, and direct the application
of statistical and quantitative analysis. Knowledge of socio-economic
trends in Canada and their impact on Statistics Canada programs.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories, principles and techniques of research are
required including: hypothesis formulation and validation; model design
and interpretation, and experimental and comparative study designs;
surveys, simulations, interviews, focus groups, file reviews and case
studies; qualitative and quantitative analysis, including statistical
sampling and cost-benefit analysis; computer assisted analytical
packages. Knowledge of the strengths and weaknesses of these methods,
tools and techniques are required in order to direct the application of
project methodologies.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the principles and practices of project management
related to human and financial resources to plan and coordinate the work
of Statistics Canada evaluation teams, and agency/ interdepartmental
project teams involved in the conduct of studies.</p>
<p>Knowledge of writing and editing skills and techniques to write
reports, presentations, correspondence and other supporting materials
and to determine and assess the design and content of reports that will
be published.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual Knowledge:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of Statistics Canada’s structure, organization, mandate,
vision, business lines, program structure, organizational hierarchy and
priorities in order to: identify evaluation issues and manage program
evaluations; direct the provision of meaningful results on individual
evaluations; provide input to the strategic and operational business
plans; and develop recommendations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of central agency policies and guidelines concerning
program evaluation in order to recommend the objectives and approaches
to program evaluations and negotiate with central agency officials
concerning the information required for evaluation. Knowledge is
required of policies, practices and guidelines related to central
agencies and Parliament accountability and reporting requirements. This
knowledge is required to recommend evaluation and performance
measurement frameworks for Statistics Canada.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the <em>Statistics Act</em> to understand the
legislative requirements under which the agency operates such as the
need to ensure the confidentiality of all information provided to
it.</p>
<p>Knowledge of other federal department programs that are affected by,
or have an impact on Statistics Canada programs and policies in order to
understand and evaluate the context in which Statistics Canada and its
programs operate and to collaborate on, manage and coordinate
inter-departmental evaluation studies and projects.</p>
<p>Knowledge of partners and stakeholders (including various provincial
or territorial statistical organizations) who are affected by or who
have an impact on departmental programs and policies to evaluate their
impact on the agency, when managing and coordinating program
evaluations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of emerging trends and developments in the Canadian private
sector, academia and non-governmental organizations, as well as
statistical programs of foreign governments in order to facilitate
appropriate analysis.</p>
<p>Knowledge of government and departmental regulations, policies and
legislation as they apply to evaluation program activities, issues,
processes and methods. These include the Public Service Values and
Ethics Code, the <em>Policy on Results</em> and its related directives
and standards (Mandatory Procedures for Departmental Results Frameworks,
Program Inventories, Performance Information Profiles and Programs,
Mandatory Procedures for Evaluation, Standard on Evaluation, Standard on
Mandatory Outcomes and Performance Indicators, Standard on Tagging),
the <em>Financial Administration Act</em>, the <em>Federal
Accountability Act</em>, Privacy and Access to Information
Acts, <em>Official Languages Act</em>, Public Opinion Research and the
Management Accountability Framework. This is required to understand the
impact of legislation, regulations and interrelationships with other
federal / provincial / territorial legislation.</p>
<p><strong>Research and Analysis:</strong></p>
<p>Directs the assessment and selection of methodologies (cost-benefit,
comparative analysis) and analytical tools (statistical analysis,
qualitative analysis) and makes recommendations on the scope and
objectives of studies to the Evaluation Manager.</p>
<p>Manages the analysis and synthesis of information and data collected
from a variety of sources and methods (surveys, interviews, literature
reviews, program documentation, operational data, statistical databases
and reports). Based on analyses, formulates conclusions on policy /
program effectiveness and develops options and recommendations for
improvement.</p>
<p>Designs, develops, tests, modifies and applies new data collection
tools, techniques and methods (databases, questionnaires, surveys, case
studies, self-assessment, interview, file review and project review
guides) and performance measurement methods to meet requirements for
unique / specialized projects.</p>
<p>Identifies sources of information required to support program
evaluation and to develop, coordinate and maintain data on
Statistics Canada programs. Identify and evaluate the extent to which
factors, including social, economic and demographic trends have impacts
on Statistics Canada programs and evaluation studies.</p>
<p>Reviews, evaluates and edits findings and conclusions of studies
conducted by project teams and/or contractors for thoroughness,
credibility, logic and validity; evaluates the appropriateness of
recommendations and advice in an environment of changing priorities and
resource constraints. Conclusions drawn and presented must also
reconcile potentially differing views of stakeholders.</p></td>

<th>EC-06</th>
<td>Specialist, Data Analytics and Integration</td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Manages activities related to: data integration, data
manipulation, data analytics, processing, analysis and the preparation
of dissemination and data visualization products. This involves setting
objectives; leading program development, research or analysis;
implementation of various tools or software, and ensuring overall
quality and completeness of program results.</p></li>
<li><p>Responsible for outreach and collaboration with a variety of
stakeholders (within the Agency, the federal, provincial-territorial or
municipal governments and agencies, academia and international
institutions and private organizations) in order to establish
relationships to leverage new methods and data sources, understand user
needs and keep abreast of economic trends and developments, and
providing expert advice and analysis.</p></li>
<li><p>Leads complex projects involving data acquisition, integration,
linkage and use of alternative data sources to respond to user needs;
develops mitigation strategies to address delays and briefs senior
management when cost or scope changes are necessary.</p></li>
<li><p>Authors analytical papers and other types of material supporting
data development and dissemination.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in or leads data acquisition negotiations.</p></li>
<li><p>Supervises directly or in a matrix environment to plan and
coordinate teams responsible for statistical outputs or
projects.</p></li>
<li></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Responsibility</strong></p>
<p><strong>Human Resources Responsibility</strong></p>
<p>Manages teams involved in the production, integration or analysis of
data and/or leads project teams. Determines objectives and priorities
and assesses the work requirements, schedules the work, resolves
conflicting priorities, ascertains human resource needs, determines the
appropriate selection methods (internal and external resources /
contractors) to meet program and project objectives. This could involve
recruitment activities, completion of employee performance evaluation or
review of performance evaluations prepared by colleagues and approval of
training plans. Present classification and staffing options to
management and coordinate some human resources activities related to
managing staff and leading a team.</p>
<p><strong>Operational Responsibility</strong></p>
<p>Responsible for promoting the development of new methods for
collecting, modeling and processing data to meet program requirements
and user needs.</p>
<p>Plans, coordinates and executes outreach activities and user
consultations.</p>
<p>Coordinates activities including: gathering information from various
sources, performing statistical analysis, interpreting and reporting on
the intelligence obtained from data analysis.</p>
<p>Responsible for the development of recommendations and providing
strategic advice to senior management in order to secure funding for
projects and change strategies related to programs.</p>
<p>Analyzes current work performed by programs or other data sources, in
order to add value by linking existing data and to expand analytical
outputs.</p>
<p>Responsible for providing advisory and consultative services as well
as subject matter advice to data users within and outside the Agency in
order to maximize the accuracy and effectiveness of data products.</p>
<p>Responsible for making linkages across programs and divisions to
leverage opportunities for further collaboration.</p>
<p>Responsible for the review and editing of analytical material for
internal use or publication purposes.</p>
<p>Synthesizes complex statistical data from a wide range of sources to
provide decision-makers at all levels of government, businesses,
national economic agencies and academics, with information leading to
decision making regarding a vast array of economic policies, programs
and initiatives.</p>
<p><strong>Skill</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge and experience using various tools, techniques or software
to evaluate, manipulate, , integrate, process, analyze and visualize
data. This may include experience with machine learning and other skills
related to big data.</p>
<p>In-depth knowledge of statistical principles, theories and practices,
to conduct comprehensive analysis for the purpose of producing reliable
and relevant statistical data, and to provide expert advisory and
consultative services to clients on program outputs, concepts and
methods used.</p>
<p>Knowledge of quantitative analytical methods required to integrate
and interpret data and to provide technical advice to other
professionals.</p>
<p>Knowledge of project management principles, including developing
project scope, budget, resource allocation, objectives, timelines, as
well as mitigation strategies to address potential delays, issues and
risks to overall project completion.</p>
<p>Knowledge of data sources, conceptual frameworks, and statistical
methods to plan, evaluate, develop and lead new or existing programs
(e.g. develop new methods, concepts, applications).</p>
<p>Ability to communicate efficiently orally and in writing (e.g.
analytical material, briefing notes, presentations), to understand user
needs, to identify data linkage opportunities, to interpret results and
synthesize messages, and to negotiate, consult and liaise with
stakeholders.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and other relevant acts related to
the subject matter areas, and of Statistics Canada policies.</p>
<p>Knowledge of statistical programs and products, including
opportunities, limitations and potential policy impacts related to the
area of expertise is required to plan and implement statistical
activities, to integrate conceptually different data from other
programs, to explain variances, and to recommend changes to statistical
programs if necessary.</p>
<p>Solid understanding of government key priorities and programs and the
ability to interpret information needs relating to specific policies or
programs of key partners.</p>
<p>Knowledge of current and emerging socio-economic issues in Canada and
abroad as they relate to statistical programs and new data
requirements.</p>
<p>Continuous learning of methods to integrate data from a variety of
sources, evaluate the conceptual framework used and the compatibility of
data with other programs, develop modifications or update program
content and coverage and devise new classifications, concepts,
definitions and operational procedures relating to area of
expertise.</p>
<p>In-depth knowledge of horizontal topics, classification systems,
concepts and international frameworks to integrate multiple data sources
and ensure the overall coherence and soundness of the economic
statistical program data produced.</p>
<p>Ability to interpret the relative quality of data produced, determine
reasons for variances, its comparability over time and its relevancy in
light of changing requirements, trends, developments, policy issues and
economic conditions.</p></td>
</tr>

<th>EC-06</th>
<td>Unit Head, Metadata Development, Dissemination and Evaluation</td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Plans, develops, implements, directs and monitors statistical
metadata projects and short-term and long-term strategies for the
development, operation and enhancement of statistical metadata
management processes and systems.</p></li>
<li><p>Determines changes in the needs of clients, assessing the impacts
of these changes on statistical metadata programs and projects; develops
options and strategies to manage complex issues and multi-stakeholder
relations; and establishes short-term and long-term plans and
priorities.</p></li>
<li><p>Identifies and researches trends and developments in the area of
statistical metadata and the methods used to develop, manipulate, store,
retrieve and disseminate statistical metadata in government and other
organizations in order to plan, manage and monitor the development,
implementation and operation of new or enhanced metadata management
processes and systems.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input to the development and revision of policies,
directives and guidelines relevant to statistical metadata quality;
plans and directs metadata quality assessment projects and reviews;
reports to senior management on the overall quality, completeness,
coherence and consistency of statistical metadata contained within
corporate metadata systems and suggests means for improvements, if
required.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in, and leads, working groups, task forces and
committees as an expert in the development, production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata in various roles
such as Project Manager, Task Manager or Senior Advisor.</p></li>
<li><p>Manages a unit and multi-disciplinary project teams involved the
development, production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata and statistical metadata processes and systems. Manages the
financial resources attached to each project.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Provides expert advice and specialized training with respect to the
policies, directives, guidelines, methods, procedures and systems used
in the production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata to subordinates, other statistical metadata specialists,
systems specialists, clients and managers who use the information as
input to on-going operations and to decisions concerning the management
of statistical metadata at Statistics Canada and within other national
and international organizations and agencies.</p>
<p>Liaises with other statistical metadata specialists, systems
specialists, clients and managers to plan statistical metadata projects
and implement short-term and long-term strategies for the development
and enhancement of policies, procedures, methods and systems used in the
management of statistical metadata. Provides direction with respect to
establishing project plans, priorities and target dates; to negotiating
modifications to methods, procedures and timelines; and to suggesting
improvements to methods, procedures and systems.</p>
<p>Provides clear and comprehensible guidance, direction and specialized
training to subordinate staff, colleagues, clients, systems specialists
and management with respect to translating requirements for the
management of statistical metadata into methods, procedures and systems
specifications for the production, storage, dissemination and evaluation
of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Writes detailed reports and prepares and delivers presentations which
explain the rationale, requirements and results relating to policies,
directives, guidelines, methods, procedures and systems used in the
production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata and which are used by other statistical metadata specialists,
systems specialists, clients, and management in the operation and
enhancement of statistical metadata management programs.  When
recommendations are challenged or misinterpreted, logical arguments are
presented to defend the validity of the approaches used in order to
maintain the division’s and Statistics Canada’s reputation for
producing, storing and disseminating high quality metadata.</p>
<p>Chairs intra-departmental meetings and participates in
inter-departmental meetings, conferences and groups to develop
understanding, consensus and share knowledge on metadata management.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Extensive knowledge of statistical metadata concepts and frameworks
used within Canada and internationally and knowledge of how those
concepts and frameworks are used and reflected in policies, methods and
procedures relating to the production, storage, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata in order to plan, develop, implement,
direct, monitor and explain statistical metadata projects and short-term
and long-term strategies for the development, operation and enhancement
of statistical metadata management processes and systems.</p>
<p>Extensive knowledge of methods, procedures and systems used for
producing, storing, disseminating and evaluating the quality of
statistical metadata and the potential impacts of new or modified
methods, procedures and systems on project teams and clients in order to
plan, develop, implement, direct, monitor and explain statistical
metadata projects and short-term and long-term strategies for the
development, operation and enhancement of statistical metadata
management processes and systems; to manage and monitor the development,
implementation and operation of new or enhanced metadata management
processes and systems, and to lead metadata quality evaluations for
surveys and statistical programs.</p>
<p>Knowledge of current and emerging trends and developments in the area
of statistical metadata management in Canada and internationally, in
order to evaluate Statistics Canada’s statistical metadata management
program in a broader context; to anticipate, understand needs for, plan,
manage and monitor the development, implementation and operation of new
or enhanced metadata management methods, procedures and systems.</p>
<p>Knowledge of survey and statistical program subject matter concepts,
methods and techniques relevant to the collection, processing,
certification and dissemination of data in order to interpret client
requirements for metadata production, storage and dissemination; to
determine when changes in the needs of clients will impact statistical
metadata programs and projects; to more accurately and efficiently
evaluate the quality of statistical metadata across surveys and
statistical programs; and to make recommendations to clients and
managers with respect to possible enhanced approaches that could result
in improved metadata quality.</p>
<p>Knowledge of management principles, techniques and practices in order
to effectively manage financial resources, coordinate the activities of
multi-disciplinary teams, manage performance, develop the skills, and
competencies of team members. Knowledge of the principles and practices
of project management used at Statistics Canada.</p>
<p>Knowledge of software, statistical packages and programming languages
used in the production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata and knowledge of new and emerging computer technology and
applications in order to inform the planning of resource allocation and
long-term strategies for the production, storage, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Extensive knowledge of Statistics Canada and Government of Canada
policies, directives and guidelines directly and specifically related to
the production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata, as well as other Statistics Canada policies, in order to
ensure that they are applied in the conduct of all program activities
and in order to explain policy requirements to clients and partners
within Canada and internationally.</p>
<p>Extensive knowledge of the mandate, objectives, organization,
practices, policies, culture and priorities of Statistics Canada in
order to ensure that these are reflected in the objectives and
management of all statistical metadata projects and short-term and
long-term strategies for the development and enhancement of statistical
metadata management processes and systems and to explain the context of
statistical metadata management at Statistics Canada to clients and
partners across Canada and internationally.</p>
<p>Knowledge of other statistical metadata management programs within
Statistics Canada in order to integrate conceptually and structurally
the statistical metadata required for various activities and programs
into a broader corporate metadata management system and to ensure the
overall relevance, completeness, coherence and consistency of
statistical metadata contained within corporate metadata systems at
Statistics Canada.</p>
<p>Extensive knowledge of surveys and statistical programs within
Statistics Canada in order to establish short-term and long-term
priorities for the processing, storing and dissemination of metadata
across program areas and to effectively and efficiently plan and direct
metadata quality assessment projects, certifying and reporting to senior
management on the overall quality, completeness, coherence and
consistency of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and Privacy Act is required in order
to ensure understanding and application of these Acts in the production,
storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata,
particularly with respect to the security and confidentiality of
statistical metadata and other information; to explain legislative
requirements to other departments, agencies, industry, members of the
public and the media; and to ensure that Statistics Canada meets its
commitment to ensuring confidentiality and does not release any
information that identifies an individual or group without prior
consent.</p>
<p><strong>Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Analysis is required to understand and interpret client requirements
with respect to the production, storage and dissemination of metadata,
to determine the capacity and ability of existing or modified systems to
meet the requirements of these clients and to translate client
requirements into timely and affordable services. Clients are often
uncertain of policy, procedural and systems restrictions and are
unfamiliar with the options, methods and timelines required to produce
and disseminate high quality metadata.</p>
<p>Reviews polices, guidelines, directives, methods, procedures and
systems used for managing statistical metadata within Canada and
internationally in order to determine the need for development,
modification and adaptation of corporate metadata systems at
Statistics Canada. Statistical metadata management programs are often
very complex and have been designed for specific purposes which must be
evaluated when taking into consideration the feasibility of the various
features of such programs and the potential impacts and results in terms
of Statistics Canada’s priorities for the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Analyses new methods, techniques and practices of managing
statistical metadata and the associated costs and limitations.
Identifies the availability of alternate means of producing, storing,
disseminating and evaluating statistical metadata in order to evaluate
the suitability of methods, procedures and systems with respect to the
short-term and long-term program impacts and benefits.</p>
<p>Analyses the quality of statistical metadata produced by surveys and
statistical programs at Statistics Canada; analyses gaps and
inconsistencies in metadata quality and determines reasons for variances
among program areas; reviews the comparability of metadata over time in
light of changing policy requirements, systems functionality and
changing methodologies used by statistical programs; provides senior
management with interpretations and recommendations affecting policies,
directives, guidelines, methods, procedures and systems used for the
production, storage and dissemination of statistical metadata.</p></td>
</tr>

<th>EC-06</th>
<td>Lead Data Scientist</td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Plans, manages and conducts studies and/or projects using
quantitative statistical data analysis methods, such as: data wrangling,
validation, mining, and visualization (in R/RStudio, Python, SQL, Java,
JavaScript or another software), as well as software development,
application of machine/deep learning and artificial intelligence
techniques to anticipate and solve complex business problems.</p></li>
<li><p>Plans, leads and identifies new opportunities for the
acquisition, analysis and integration of large statistical data sets
from a wide variety of structured and unstructured data sources to
identify patterns and trends contributing to the development of
solutions to complex business problems.</p></li>
<li><p>Plans, researches, leads and develops statistical measures and
new data analysis techniques and processes, survey development, survey
methodology, or components of data governance (e.g., policies, processes
and standards, privacy, data quality, risk management) and on the use of
data to optimize business value.</p></li>
<li><p>Plans and leads the assessment of the social, economic, fiscal
implications of data to ensure that the development of policies,
strategies, programs and other initiatives take into consideration the
business foresight revealed from data analytics.</p></li>
<li><p>Plans and leads the ongoing tracking, monitoring and reporting of
the performance of decision systems and accuracy of statistical
models.</p></li>
<li><p>Advises and briefs project teams, colleagues, managers and senior
managers on implications of studies conducted.  Drafts dashboards,
briefing notes, reports, and presentations, and contributes to the
development and evaluation of position and policy papers and ministerial
correspondence and/or Cabinet documents. </p></li>
<li><p>Plans, coordinates and leads Departmental project teams/working
groups and/or components of interdepartmental projects/working groups. 
Formulates and recommends the nature, scope, analytical methods,
objectives, terms of reference and resource requirements for research
projects.  Manages resources assigned to studies and projects. Develops
contracts and cost estimates to cover activities of projects. Negotiates
contracts with consultants. Monitors contractor deliverables and
recommends approval of invoices for payment.</p></li>
<li><p>Represents the Departmental position on data strategy, plans and
initiatives in consultation and discussions on collaborative data
science projects with partners including other government
departments.</p></li>
<li><p>Builds client/stakeholder networks and provides technical
expertise and leadership to federal government departments in matters
relating to data science and analytics, to optimize business value of
data, to determine the implications on policy and programs and the
decision-making process.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Responsibility</strong></p>
<p>Judgement and latitude are required to plan, manage and conduct
economic, sociological and/or statistical projects, to analyse existing
data science techniques, methods or approaches and to plan, develop and
implement databases, and other analytical tools, approaches and
methodologies.</p>
<p>There is a requirement to plan, manage/coordinate the work of unit
analysts, and/or lead Departmental projects on a regular basis, as well
as components of interdepartmental projects/working groups.</p>
<p>There is a requirement to provide advice and prepare briefing notes,
discussion, position and policy papers, and presentations for senior
managers, for use at Department and interdepartmental meetings. The
purpose is to communicate results of analysis and studies and their
implications relative to the development and/or assessment of new or
enhanced policies, regulations, legislation or other initiatives.</p>
<p>There is also a requirement to manage, plan, design and develop data
translation and exchange activities to support the sharing and use of
data internally and externally.</p>
<p>This work affects data science activities undertaken by other
analysts, multi-disciplinary working groups and/or project teams, and
ultimately decisions and recommendations made by senior management.</p>
<p>Judgement to discuss business information without divulging
confidential information.</p>
<p>Plans, manages/coordinates the work of unit analysts, and/or lead
Departmental projects on a regular basis, as well as components of
interdepartmental projects/working groups. This includes planning and
