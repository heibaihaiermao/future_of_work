<th><strong>EC-05</strong></th>
<td><strong>Evaluation Analyst</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Plans, organizes, conducts and participates in evaluation studies
related to Statistics Canada programs, policies and initiatives to
evaluate their relevance and performance, under the supervision of the
Evaluation Manager.</p></li>
<li><p>Identifies and recommends evaluation frameworks; formulates
evaluation project plans in conjunction with the Evaluation Manager and
recommends the objectives, scope, schedules, budgets, criteria,
methodologies, data gathering instruments and terms of reference of
studies; recommends study approaches, results indicators and study
designs.</p></li>
<li><p>Researches, identifies and selects information for evaluations
from a variety of sources; analyzes information related to
Statistics Canada business lines, policy, program performance, program
relevance, success and cost-effectiveness.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops and/or carries out specific elements of
Statistics Canada evaluation projects such as: conducting literature and
data research; identifying, gathering, analyzing and synthesizing
quantitative and qualitative data; participating in the conduct of
interviews and focus groups; and applying analytical tools and
techniques to determine implications of evaluation results and
formulating conclusions.</p></li>
<li><p>Assesses current and future requirements for data related to
evaluation and performance measurement; interprets and analyzes
information to support findings and conclusions concerning various
elements of Statistics Canada evaluation studies.</p></li>
<li><p>Analyzes and interprets the results of evaluation studies and
projects conducted with Statistics Canada clients, and OGDs, partners
and stakeholders in order to analyze the relevance and performance of
programs of Statistics Canada; and conducts follow-up interviews to
clarify or expand information obtained from evaluation studies.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops evaluation reports, briefings and presentations
outlining results of evaluation studies; develops options, strategies
and recommendations, and provides advice and recommendations to the
Evaluation Manager and Director to improve the effectiveness of
policies, programs and services.</p></li>
<li><p>Consults and works collaboratively with other government
departments, partners and stakeholders when conducting evaluation
studies and projects; represents Statistics Canada at internal and
external meetings to discuss various aspects of evaluation studies; and
shares best practices in the evaluation field.</p></li>
<li></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication:</strong></p>
<p>Oral and written skills are required to present and explain to
Statistics Canada program and departmental managers, the findings,
conclusions and recommendations from evaluations and other studies, in
order to gain acceptance and implementation of recommendations. The
subject matter is diverse and technical information can be complex.</p>
<p>Oral presentation skills are required to explain to Statistics Canada
program managers and staff, or project team members, issues related to
evaluation processes, practices, plans and methodologies, result-based
measurement, management, processes and practices, to obtain cooperation
throughout the project phases and ultimately to gain acceptance of study
findings, conclusions and recommendations.</p>
<p>Consultation and liaison skills are required to establish and
maintain a variety of networks within and outside of the Federal
government to participate on departmental and interdepartmental
committees, seminars, workshops and task forces with individuals from a
variety of disciplines and knowledge levels. This involves discussing
and resolving issues, working partnerships, gaining and maintaining the
acceptance of members, and developing strong working relationships.</p>
<p>Strong written skills are required to develop and prepare evaluation
reports, papers, briefing notes, presentations and Ministerial
correspondence.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of Specialized Fields:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge is required of program evaluation theories, processes and
practices for conducting evaluation studies and development of
evaluation frameworks related to studies. This knowledge is required to
plan, organize and conduct program evaluation studies and to develop,
options, strategies and recommendations based on studies conducted.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories and principles of economics, sociology, social
sciences, demography and statistics to: plan, direct and coordinate
economic and socio-economic analysis of programs to evaluate their
effectiveness against organizational strategic objectives; formulate and
provide recommendations on agency policies, and direct the application
of statistical and quantitative analysis. Knowledge of socio-economic
trends in Canada and their impact on Statistics Canada programs.</p>
<p>Knowledge of program evaluation methodologies, including planning
tools such as terms of reference, statements of work and requests for
proposals; as well as logic models, comparative analyses, profiles, case
studies, and data collection instruments (focus sessions, interviews,
and surveys). These skills are required to define and recommend
approaches to evaluations and to plan and conduct the evaluation
process.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories, principles and techniques of research is
required, including: hypothesis formulation and validation; model design
and interpretation, experimental and comparative study designs; surveys,
simulations, interviews, focus groups, file reviews and case studies;
qualitative and quantitative analysis, including statistical sampling
and cost-benefit analysis; computer assisted analysis packages; survey
questionnaire design; and the strengths and weaknesses of these methods,
tools and techniques in order to recommend and select project
methodologies.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual Knowledge:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of Statistics Canada’s structure, organization, mandate,
vision, business lines, program structure, organizational hierarchy and
priorities in order to: identify evaluation issues and conduct program
evaluations; contribute meaningful results on individual evaluations;
provide input to strategic and operational business plans; and develop
recommendations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of central agency policies and guidelines concerning
program evaluation in order to recommend the objectives and approaches
for program evaluations. Knowledge is required of policies, practices
and guidelines related to central agencies and Parliament accountability
and reporting requirements. This knowledge is required to recommend
evaluation and performance measurement frameworks.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the <em>Statistics Act</em> to understand the
legislative requirements under which the agency operates such as the
need to ensure the confidentiality of all information provided to
it.</p>
<p>Knowledge of other federal department programs that are affected by,
or have an impact on Statistics Canada programs and policies in order to
understand and evaluate the context in which Statistics Canada and its
programs operate and to collaborate on inter-departmental evaluation
studies and projects.</p>
<p>Knowledge of partners and stakeholders (including various provincial
or territorial statistical organizations) who are affected by or who
have an impact on departmental programs and policies to evaluate their
impact on the agency.</p>
<p>Knowledge of emerging trends and developments in the Canadian private
sector, academia and non-governmental organizations, as well as
statistical programs of foreign governments in order to facilitate
appropriate analysis.</p>
<p>Knowledge of government and departmental regulations, policies and
legislation as they apply to evaluation program activities, issues,
processes and methods. These include the Public Service Values and
Ethics Code, the <em>Policy on Results</em> and its related directives
and standards (Mandatory Procedures for Departmental Results Frameworks,
Program Inventories, Performance Information Profiles and Programs,
Mandatory Procedures for Evaluation, Standard on Evaluation, Standard on
Mandatory Outcomes and Performance Indicators, Standard on Tagging),
the <em>Financial Administration Act</em>, the <em>Federal
Accountability Act</em>, Privacy and Access to Information
Acts, <em>Official Languages Act</em>, Public Opinion Research and the
Management Accountability Framework. This is required to understand the
impact of legislation, regulations and interrelationships with other
federal / provincial / territorial legislation.</p>
<p><strong>Research and Analysis:</strong></p>
<p>Assesses current and future requirements for data; interprets and
analyzes information to support findings and conclusions for evaluations
conducted; analyzes, assesses and synthesizes information from a variety
of sources in order to carry out evaluations of programs and
policies.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to assimilate and integrate evaluation
studies to arrive at innovative solutions and recommendations for future
actions and direction, including improvements to Statistics Canada
policies, programs and services.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to analyze the results of studies and
projects conducted with Statistics Canada’s clients, OGDs, partners and
stakeholders to examine relevance and performance of programs.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to recommend evaluation frameworks and
terms of reference for evaluations, which includes the need to
conceptualize and identify potential evaluation issues and results
indicators, and propose appropriate study designs. As well, there is a
requirement to quickly understand the key elements of the program or
initiative, to the extent that a solid working hypothesis can be
developed, and to remain flexible to the evolving information flow and
potential shifts in direction as the project proceeds.</p>
<p>The scope of work is determined in conjunction with the Evaluation
Manager. There is a requirement to determine information to be utilized
in evaluation studies. Information is obtained from a variety of sources
including: interviews and focus groups with departmental managers,
employees and stakeholders; Acts and Regulations as well as
socio-economic and scientific documents; publications and conferences
related to policy and government machinery issues. There is a
requirement to recommend areas for further review based on studies
conducted.</p>
<p>Adapts methods and techniques used in program evaluation to meet
requirements. There is also a need to determine the impact of variables
on studies conducted. Such variables include: emerging trends and
developments in the provincial / territorial data systems, as well as
developments in the Canadian private sector, academia and NGOs.</p></td>

<th><strong>EC-05</strong></th>
<td><strong>Planning and Research Analyst</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Conducts research and analysis into economic trends, policy and
initiative issues, reviews planning and management policies and
standards and submits program evaluation reports and documents.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides consultative services to departmental clients related to
strategic, annual and long-term evaluation plans, priority setting,
strategic planning, policy development, performance measurement,
monitoring and reporting.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops and recommends strategies to assist departmental
managers to effectively integrate program evaluation methodologies into
the design and implementation of programs, policies, and
initiatives.<br />
<br />
The Planning, Evaluation and Resource Management Division is the Office
of Modern Comptrollership which provides direction and expertise on the
development and implementation of  the MAF (Management Accountability
Framework).<br />
<br />
Prepares reports on business requirements along with recommendations on
alternative solutions to meet those requirements and provides assistance
in the design of appropriate business and financial planning processes,
strategies and plans to support the integration of strategic business
needs.</p></li>
<li><p>Conducts evaluation projects to assess the impact, effectiveness,
efficiency and relevance of departmental and interdepartmental programs,
policies and initiatives and participates in the Strategic Streamlining
Process by helping managers identify and achieve program
efficiencies.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops evaluation standards and performance indicators as well
as procedures for monitoring and reporting purposes.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Job Content Knowledge Application<br />
<br />
</strong>Knowledge of statistical methods and techniques to conduct
research and analysis into economic trends, policy and initiative
issues; to collect and analyze foundation data in order to create new
information about programs and their effectiveness; to select data
sources to be used; and to develop and gather information which will be
used by management to make informed decisions concerning the scope,
structure, redefinition or termination of major programs, initiatives,
operations or activities.</p>
<p>Knowledge of concepts, methods and techniques to develop evaluation
standards and performance indicators as well as procedures for
monitoring and reporting purposes.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of computer software applications to conduct
analysis and document project results, including specialized software
such as statistical sampling, computer assisted techniques, automated
working papers, and flowcharting.</p>
<p>Knowledge of social science research methods, techniques and
practices, including multi-disciplinary analysis methods, to carry out
analysis on a variety of approaches regarding performance and
accountability framework in other levels of government, other
jurisdictions and in the private sector.</p>
<p>Skill in synthesising information from diverse disciplines
(management science, economics, and public policy administration, etc.)
to facilitate the development and the implementation of
interdepartmental performance frameworks.<br />
Knowledge of theories, principles and techniques of consultation,
management, conflict resolution, small group process, interviewing and
counselling to lead project teams, to negotiate contracts for services
and agreements with other institutions.<strong><br />
<br />
Contextual Knowledge<br />
OWN WORK UNIT:<br />
</strong>Knowledge is required of the organization and objectives of the
evaluation division; the strategic direction, culture, structure and
organization, mandate, objectives, policies, procedures and work tools
of the Field, branch, division; and the structure and organization of
project work teams, their mandates and objectives. This knowledge is
required to achieve operational objectives and results in an effective
and efficient manner, and to recommend new or improved business
services, policies, systems and procedures.<br />
<br />
Knowledge of the policies, procedures, guidelines, mandate and personnel
of the Branch to provide consultative services related to strategic,
annual and long-term evaluation plans, priority setting, strategic
planning, policy development, performance measurement, monitoring and
reporting; and to develop and recommend strategies to assist
departmental managers to effectively integrate program evaluation
methodologies into the design and implementation of programs, policies,
and initiatives.<strong><br />
<br />
OWN DEPARTMENT OR AGENCY:<br />
</strong>Knowledge of the policies, procedures, guidelines, mandate and
personnel of the department to prepare program evaluation reports and
documents on the economy, efficiency and effectiveness of existing
programs, operations and initiatives; to monitor and assess management
accountability for departmental programs; and to recommend ways to
improve departmental policies, programs and operations.<strong><br />
<br />
OTHER FEDERAL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES:<br />
</strong>Knowledge of the personnel, organization, priorities, policies,
mandate, objectives, goals and programs of a wide range of client
departments and organizations to provide advice and guidance regarding
the development and implementation of reporting and performance review
frameworks, processes and tools.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of the Parliamentary system and machinery of
government, and its expectations for the Department, policy making and
legislative processes, government priorities, and  current and emerging
management  issues; the mandate, policies, programs, operations and
reporting processes of Parliamentary committees, central agencies, and
other federal government departments; and the role of Treasury Board and
the Privy Council Office. </p>
<p><strong>CANADIAN PRIVATE SECTOR AND OTHER PUBLIC SECTORS:<br />
</strong>Knowledge of personnel and organisation of different orders of
government (provincial, territorial, municipal), the academic community,
and non-government organisations in order to effectively communicate on
performance and accountability approaches, methods, theories, studies,
and analysis for the purpose of strengthening performance reporting to
Parliament and the public.<br />
<br />
Knowledge of think tanks that are working in the area of citizen
engagement, societal indicators and quality of life modelling, to carry
out analysis regarding alternative performance and accountability
frameworks and approaches (e.g. National Statistical
Council).<strong><br />
<br />
INTERNATIONAL PRIVATE AND PUBLIC SECTOR:<br />
</strong>Knowledge of personnel and organisation of different national

the purpose of strengthening performance reporting to Parliament and the

<br />
FLEGISLATION AND REGULATIONS:<br />
</strong>Knowledge of certain sections of legislation related to client

<p><strong><br />
COMMUNICATION IN:<br />
</strong>Listening and evaluation skills to interpret verbal and

are, to retain salient points and synthesize information on a variety of
issues of differing complexity, and to understand opinion and points of
view.<br />
Reading and listening skills, to maintain knowledge of key developments,
research and innovations related to reporting and accountability
processes and techniques, to attend conferences and meetings of
professional associations related to auditing, financial analysis, and
accountability reporting, to analyse and assess a wide range of
alternative accountability approaches, strategies and processes, to

monitoring and reporting.</p>
<p>Writing skills to prepare program evaluation reports and documents on
the economy, efficiency and effectiveness of existing programs,
operations and initiatives.</p>
<p>Speaking and writing skills are required to explain and clarify
issues, to present arguments, report findings and recommendations to
senior officials and stakeholders, and to adapt large volumes of
information and specialized terminology in order to improve departmental
policies, programs and operations.  </p>
<p><strong>Motor and Sensory Skills<br />
</strong>Dexterity and co-ordination skills are required on a daily
basis to operate a computer keyboard and mouse to search, retrieve, and
manipulate and transform information and data from information bases,
the Internet and Intranet as well as to type briefing notes, memoranda,
notes, reports, and analytical documents.  Any requirement for
proficiency, precision and speed in the conduct of this activity is
under the control of the incumbent.</p></td>

<th><strong>EC-02</strong></th>
<td><strong>Junior Data Analyst</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Supports the conduct of studies and/or projects using established
quantitative statistical data analysis methods and software such as
Python, SAS, STATA, R, E-Views, SPSS or other similar software.</p></li>
<li><p>Conducts data preparation techniques, for example in data
extraction, data cleansing, data transformation, data validation,
ascertaining data quality, conformity to standards, or data
origin.</p></li>
<li><p>Organizes and consolidates information relevant to the defined
objectives of studies. Maintains work files.</p></li>
<li><p>Attends Departmental meetings, and interdepartmental and
stakeholder meetings (as required), to gather data/information.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in project teams and working groups to support data
management, research and analysis, and other unit activities.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p>Communication skills are required to maintain networks across the
branch and the federal community of practice to share best practices in
advanced analytics.</p>
<p>Provides advice, information and guidance to data users and clients
regarding governance concepts used to protect and optimize the utility
of Statistics Canada’s data assets.</p>
<p>Writing skills are necessary to draft clear and simple analytical
reports, briefings and presentation material using data visualization
tools for managers and clients with varying levels of knowledge and
understanding of data science. Information must be impartial and clearly
presented to minimize the potential for misinterpretation.</p>
<p>As assigned, discusses data requirements with operations managers
responsible for data collection, other subject matter and systems
specialists, clients or service providers to comprehend and elaborate
the conditions within which statistical development and operations will
occur.</p>
<p>There is a requirement to liaise with other subject matter
specialists and users to develop and translate subject matter
requirements into specifications for data development, and to suggest
improvements to ongoing programs.</p>
<p>The work requires knowledge of data science and its life cycle,
including data capture and acquisition, data extraction, warehousing,
cleansing, processing, mining, clustering, modeling, summarization,
reporting, visualization, business intelligence, and exploratory/
confirmatory, predictive analysis, regression, text mining, and
qualitative analysis. This knowledge is required to optimize the use of
the Statistics Canada’s data assets and to provide leading edge thinking
and methodologies to data analytics.</p>
<p>The work requires knowledge of the theories, principles, practices,
and methodologies of statistics and mathematics in order to conduct the
requisite modelling that provides meaningful information for policy and
program decisions by management.</p>
<p>Knowledge of techniques and methods of information collection and
analysis, evolving information sources, survey methodologies, data risk
assessment, impact and quantitative analysis in order to assess the
quality of data and identify and mitigate risks to its integrity.</p>
<p>Data literacy skills are needed to derive meaningful information from
data, including the knowledge needed to read, analyze, interpret,
visualize and communicate data, as well as understand the use of data in
decision-making. Data literacy includes data stewardship and the ability
to assess the quality of data, protect and secure data, and safeguard
their responsible use. This is needed to develop briefings,
presentations and dashboards that contain valid and reliable data.</p>
<p>Basic knowledge of theories, principles and techniques of research is
required including; hypothesis formulation and validation; model design
and interpretation, experimental and comparative study designs; surveys,
simulations, file reviews and case studies; qualitative and quantitative
analysis including statistical sampling and cost benefit analysis;
computer assisted analysis packages; and the strengths and weaknesses of
these methods, tools and techniques in order to recommend and select
project methodologies.</p>
<p>The work requires a basic knowledge of the mandate, vision, values,
culture, organization, legislation, programs, business lines and
operations of Statistics Canada. This is needed to ensure the alignment
of the data analytics strategy with the department’s business
intelligence requirements.</p>
<p>The work requires knowledge and understanding of the governance
structures of Statistics Canada’s partnerships in order to assess data
provided by other levels of government for validity and integrity.</p>
<p>Basic knowledge of Departmental, Central Agencies and Library and
Archives Canada policies pertaining to the handling, tracking, filing
and storing of sensitive and confidential material and information
holdings, including electronic records.</p></td>
</tr>

<th><strong>EC-05</strong></th>
<td><strong>Production and Database Expert</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Innovates and implements the design, evaluation, and applications
for the manipulation, storage, and retrieval of high-impact statistical
data.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops and maintains production and dissemination processes and
outputs for analytical databases and provides support for tools and
systems for the delivery of high-impact statistical programs, new
products, special studies, and ad hoc requests.</p></li>
<li><p>Coordinates activities of multiple Statistic Canada divisional
partners and other external partners to process, validate, and
disseminate statistical data.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides specialized expertise and consultative services
regarding production systems and processes for ongoing statistical
program delivery.</p></li>
<li><p>Represents economic subject matter divisions on statistical
development projects.</p></li>
<li><p>Occasionally leads project teams and undertakes complex technical
projects in the development and implementation of new or modified
statistical processing systems.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Decision Making</strong></p>
<p>Integrates input from multiple economic subject matter areas to
develop and maintain complex systems and frameworks for the ongoing
production, validation and dissemination of a range of specialized,
high-impact statistical outputs.</p>
<p>Operationalizes specialized conceptual and measurement frameworks in
the development of production, validation and dissemination systems,
processes and databases.</p>
<p>Evaluates alternatives and develops and applies new strategies to
maintain and improve operational systems, processes and databases in the
production and dissemination of official statistics. Decisions and
recommendations regarding data production and dissemination practices
impact multiple projects and high-impact statistical programs.</p>
<p>Develops and implements quality-assurance processes for high-impact
statistical outputs.</p>
<p>Investigates requirements for statistical information and analysis,
defines the nature and scope of special studies, data requirements, and
decides the methods and approaches to be used to satisfy complex data
requirements. Modifies existing techniques or develops new approaches to
meet specific needs.</p>
<p>Defines project objectives and priorities, prepares work plans and
summaries outlining approaches, time-frames and resources.  Each project
is unique in nature and decisions impact on other ongoing studies.</p>
<p>Develops guidelines for the design, evaluation, and testing of
processing systems and applications and recommends and implements
modifications and improvements to existing procedures.</p>
<p>Recommends and executes solutions to resolve complex technical issues
pertaining to data production and dissemination that may lack
precedents.</p>
<p>Develops data bases for the provision of time-series information,
interprets preliminary results, reviews, and modifies approaches and
techniques as necessary.</p>
<p>Reviews and approves detailed processing parameters, devises criteria
and methods to rectify inconsistencies, and recommends modifications to
ensure the continuity and compatibility of data.</p>
<p><strong>Leadership And Operational Management</strong></p>
<p>Occasionally plans, organizes and functionally coordinates the work
of multiple partners within Statistic Canada divisions and units, as
well as clients from other federal government departments involved in
the conduct of statistical processing, validation and dissemination of
specialized statistical outputs.  Determines goals, priorities and
milestones, sets objectives, plans and schedules the work and resolves
conflicting priorities.</p>
<p>Establishes and coordinates schedules for the production, validation
and dissemination of regular statistical outputs across multiple
production teams.</p>
<p>Coordinates input and specifications from multiple partners to
implement specialized solutions according to established production
schedules in the context of complex statistical frameworks. </p>
<p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Provides expert advice and guidance to internal partners on
statistical program results, dissemination methods, concepts, and
techniques applied and on trends and developments in economic or related
data programs. This information is used as input to decision making for
a vast array of economic programs and other activities.</p>
<p>Interprets specialized information pertaining to a range of
specialized subject matter areas to present arguments and options to
clients within and outside the department.</p>
<p>Communicates subject matter concepts and technical definitions to
internal contacts and other subject matter experts to explain and
clarify current developments, to explain the results of studies, and to
resolve differences that can affect the efficiency of the manipulation,
storage, and retrieval of data and the quality of outputs.</p>
<p><strong>Specialized knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Advanced knowledge of economic statistical frameworks, principles,
and practices as they relate to a specialized subject matter area.</p>
<p>Advanced knowledge of theories, concepts, and principles in
statistical measurement, and how they interrelate with at least one
specialized economic subject matter area or discipline, is required to
design, evaluate, and innovate systems and applications for the
production, validation, and dissemination of high-impact statistical
outputs. This knowledge is also required to provide subject-matter
expertise, advice, and consultative services to Statistic Canada
partners, as well as to work with other professionals, in the ongoing
production and validation of high-impact statistical data.</p>
<p>In-depth knowledge and mastery in the application of specialized
software, such as SAS, for the compilation, validation, and quality
assurance of key statistical outputs.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of the divisional mandate, activities, priorities,
practices, standards and procedures applicable to the work is required
to incorporate them into the conduct and management of surveys and
complex and technical studies and projects.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the mandate, objectives and organization of
Statistics Canada is required to manage subject matter studies, surveys
and projects across economic-based divisions. Knowledge of other
statistical programs (concepts, methods, and databases) related to the
area of expertise is also required to plan and implement statistical
activities, to integrate conceptually different data from other
programs, to explain variances, and to recommend changes to other
statistical programs if necessary.</p>
<p>In-depth knowledge of economics-related subject matter area(s) is
required to understand complex data needs from internal and external
data users and to deliver expert technical solutions in response to
analytical requirements for statistical projects and programs.</p>
<p>Knowledge of departmental publication, marketing and dissemination
policies and practices in order to establish common divisional policies
and practices in the preparation of statistical tables, charts,
explanatory notes etc. for dissemination to user and/or subject matter
clients.</p>
<p>Knowledge of Statistics Canada policies (confidentiality of data,
security, respondent relations, data quality), practices and
classification systems to ensure that they are applied in the conduct of
complex and technical statistical projects and activities.</p>
<p>Knowledge of data suppliers to discuss data reporting agreements.
Knowledge of data users from governments and from the private sector is
required to identify potential new markets and to understand client
needs for complex statistical data or studies.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and other pertinent acts such as the
Corporate Returns Act and Income Tax Act,  and of Statistics Canada
policies to explain legislative and policy requirements to others. 
Knowledge of the Statistic and Privacy Acts is particularly important
with respect to the security and confidentiality of information and
data provided and to ensure that they do not release any information
which may identify an individual or group without prior consent.</p>
<p><strong>Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Interprets the results of tests, procedural investigations, and
feasibility studies to produce data bases and data retrieval packages
used for the production of integrated statistical information.</p>
<p>Reviews and approves detailed processing parameters, classification,
coding systems and report formats or develops new ones to meet specific
needs.  Some studies may involve a combination of statistical techniques
and methods and the integration of data from a wide variety of sources
(e.g. financial statements, tax files, other administrative sources,
surveys, censuses). Often, the data has been collected for other
purposes using different definitions or concepts and must be translated
into common concepts.</p>
<p>Reviews new and revised processing systems and procedures to ensure
compatibility with other statistical programs. Assesses technical and
conceptual problems and inconsistencies in the manipulation, storage and
retrieval of statistical data and develops specifications for new and
existing data compilation programs.</p>
<p>Evaluates the impact of technological developments on operation and
production procedures and prepares reports recommending appropriate
approaches and guidelines.</p>
<p>Research and validation is required to evaluate the effectiveness of
the processing systems, to formulate computer systems specifications, to
design and create test procedures and acceptance criteria, and to ensure
that processing systems yield desired statistical outputs.</p></td>
</tr>

relevant statistical data, and to provide subject-matter expertise,

<p>Basic knowledge of project management methods, principles and
techniques for developing project objectives and timelines.</p>

data.</p>

outputs.</p>
<p>Ability to communicate efficiently orally and in writing (i.e.

needs, to identify data linkage opportunities, to interpret results and
synthesize messages.</p>

<p>Knowledge of divisional and organizational mandate, activities,

programs, to explain variances and to recommend changes to statistical
programs if necessary.</p>

initiatives.</p>

<p>Understanding of data suppliers and users, their needs and the

<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and other relevant acts related to

multilateral organizations or briefing notes from other meetings

progress on on-going activities.</p>
