<th><strong>EC-03</strong></th>
<td><strong>Evaluation Officer</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Participates as a member of project teams, in the conduct of
evaluation studies related to Statistics Canada programs, policies and
initiatives, to evaluate their relevance and performance, under the
supervision of the Evaluation Manager.</p></li>
<li><p>Develops and / or carries out some elements of Statistics Canada
evaluation projects such as, conducting literature and data research;
identifying, gathering, analyzing and synthesizing quantitative and
qualitative data; participating in the conduct of interviews and focus
groups; applying analytical tools and techniques to determine
implications of evaluation results and formulating conclusions.</p></li>
<li><p>Assesses current requirements for data; interprets and analyzes
information and databases to support findings and conclusions concerning
various elements of Statistics Canada evaluations.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input on the interpretation of data to support findings
and conclusions concerning various elements of Statistics Canada
evaluations. Recommends to the Evaluation Manager, enhancements to
methods for retrieving data as well as information used in the conduct
of socio-economic analyses and cost-benefit analyses.</p></li>
<li><p>Contributes to the drafting of reports, presentations, briefing
notes and memoranda for senior evaluation staff and management on
elements of program evaluation related to the various Statistics Canada
programs.</p></li>
<li><p>Attends departmental and inter-departmental meetings, when
required, to discuss files, issues and evaluation requirements;
interprets and reports on trends; and analyzes the effect of new or
revised policies concerning program evaluation.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication:</strong></p>
<p>Written skills are required to synthesize, clarify and interpret
complex, technical and sensitive information relating to
Statistics Canada program evaluations, and to summarize information
obtained while participating in studies and submitting data/information
related to evaluation reports for review by analysts at more senior
levels. There is a requirement to present information concisely and
clearly.</p>
<p>Written skills are required to draft parts of reports, briefing notes
and presentations dealing with complex and potentially sensitive issues.
This involves the requirement to explain, clarify and interpret
technical information related to Statistics Canada program evaluation to
improve understanding of senior colleagues, senior management and
departmental officials.</p>
<p>Oral presentation skills are required to provide briefings concerning
evaluation issues, processes and methodologies, to managers and
colleagues. Liaison and consultation skills are required to maintain
client / stakeholder networks and foster collaborative working
relationships with clients and stakeholders, as well as, to conduct
interviews.</p>
<p>Oral communication skills are required to participate in the conduct
of interviews, focus groups, and discussions with team members to
provide updates on the status of evaluation project activities, review
findings and discuss potential recommendations.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of Specialized Fields:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of theories and principles of economics, socio-economic,
demography and statistics to: participate in the conduct of evaluation
studies relating to Statistics Canada programs, policies and
initiatives; be a member of project teams and interpret and analyze
information and databases related to various elements of
Statistics Canada evaluations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories, methods and techniques of research, including
social science research and analysis in order to: undertake literature
and data research in support of Statistics Canada program evaluation
projects; identify, synthesize and analyze qualitative and quantitative
research data; conduct interviews and participate in focus groups.</p>
<p>Knowledge of program evaluation and performance measurement theories,
processes and practices to participate in the conduct of evaluation
studies, to make recommendations concerning the nature and scope of
studies and study approaches, and to draft and write reports, memoranda
and briefing notes based on elements of studies conducted.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual Knowledge:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge is required of the mission, mandate, organization, and
objectives of Statistics Canada accountabilities and policies. This
knowledge is required to develop and carry out specific elements of
evaluation studies.</p>
<p>Knowledge is required of departmental legislation of
Statistics Canada, central agencies, Parliament accountability and
reporting requirements, government and departmental regulations,
policies and legislation as they apply to evaluation program activities,
issues, processes and methods, such as the Public Service Values and
Ethics Code, the <em>Policy on Results, Privacy and Access to
Information Acts</em>. This knowledge is required to participate in
Statistics Canada program evaluations and studies.</p>
<p>Basic knowledge is required of mandate and evaluation policies of the
central agencies related to program evaluation, Statistics Canada, and
government priorities for Statistics Canada.</p>
<p><strong>Research and Analysis:</strong></p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to select, identify and utilize
information for studies, from a variety of sources, including
interviews, Statistics Canada documents and those of other government
departments, information from provincial and territorial governments, as
well as internet sources of information. The incumbent must also analyze
and synthesize quantitative and qualitative research data, and apply
analytical tools and techniques in order to determine the impact of
evaluation studies.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to conduct assessments of current and
future requirements for data; interpreting and analyzing information /
databases to support findings and conclusions concerning various
elements of Statistics Canada evaluations conducted.</p>
<p>Analytical skills are required to contribute input to the Evaluation
Manager on the extent, nature, scope, objectives and approaches to
evaluation studies, modifications to analytical methods and techniques
to be used in studies and success indicators.</p></td>

<td><strong>Library Technician  3</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Acquires the full range of print, non-print and electronic
information resources for the Library and the department and maintains
all related files.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in collection development activities, including
planning, information audits, product evaluation and usage
reports.</p></li>
<li><p>Conducts system administration / problem analysis of the
integrated library system; manages the Library intranet site and Library
wiki.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides problem resolution in the integrated library system
which includes adding patches, system upgrades and
configuration.</p></li>
<li><p>Leads project teams involved in the provision of library services
and products.</p></li>
<li><p>Trains Library staff on integrated library system
functionalities.</p></li>
<li><p>storing, disseminating and evaluating statistical metadata; and
participates in the testing of new procedures and systems.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input to documentation, procedures manuals and training
manuals related to the production and dissemination of statistical
metadata and assists with the delivery of training.</p></li>
<li><p>Under guidance, assists with statistical metadata quality
evaluations and provides input to reports for clients and
management.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates on teams tasked with the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>COMMUNICATION</strong></p>
<p>Responds to requests from federal government employees, clients or
colleagues, using language that is easily understood, whether orally or
in writing.</p>
<p>Requests input, information or approval of existing information from
internal clients, vendors and employees in other federal government
departments, using language that is clearly understood, whether orally
or in writing.</p>
<p>Prepares reports (including financial and usage) for a range of
clients both internal and external to the Library in response to
requests for information.</p>
<p>Prepares and delivers reports to library management for analysis and
decision making. </p>
<p>Writes information products, draft procedures, correspondence,
reports, training material and memoranda for clients, colleagues and
management. </p>
<p><strong>4. KNOWLEDGE OF SPECIALIZED FIELDS</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of the practices, techniques and standards (e.g.,
Anglo-American Cataloguing Rules, 2nd edition, MAchine Readable
Cataloguing) related to a specialized area within the Library in order
to provide services and/or products.</p>
<p>Knowledge of accounting techniques and financial procedures (e.g.,
commitment of funds, pre‑payment, deposit accounts, group orders,
discounts) to manage an acquisitions budget (e.g., track expenditures,
compare estimated and actual prices, estimate budget surpluses and
deficits) and to choose vendors and process orders.</p>
<p>Knowledge of basic UNIX, Perl scripting language, API programming and
HTML coding to correct problems that occur with the integrated library
system and to develop/make improvements/enhancements to the system.</p>
<p>Knowledge of automated library systems to evaluate, test, implement,
assess system requirements, to provide input for Requests for Proposals
and maintain the integrated library system.</p>
<p>Writing and presentation skills are required to deliver training or
instruction to clients and staff.</p>
<p><strong>5. CONTEXTUAL KNOWLEDGE</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of the divisional mandate, activities, priorities,
policies, standards, practices, procedures, integrated library system
and Statistics Canada’s online catalogue to effectively deliver library
services to the library’s clientele, both internal and external to the
department.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the role, responsibilities and long term objectives of
the department, its organization, programs, and regional activities to
understand and fulfill information requirements.</p>
<p>Knowledge of departmentally supported software (e.g., word
processing, spreadsheets, communication, Internet) to locate, assess and
disseminate information.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the use of databases used within the Library to create
and modify records, to manipulate and extract data and to produce
reports for departmental clients.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the acts and regulations related to the services of the
Library and the department, including but not limited to the Statistics
Act, Library and Archives of Canada Act, Copyright Act and Financial
Administration Act to provide information to internal and external
clients and to comply with audit requirements.</p>
<p>Knowledge of administrative principles, contract management and
project management. This knowledge is required to process / manage
internal transactions such as journal vouchers and contract
renewals.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the information industry and technological trends to
improve library services and reduce costs by adapting new technologies,
developing new services and/or recommending changes to current
practices/policies.</p>
<p><strong>6.  RESEARCH AND ANALYSIS</strong></p>
<p>Investigates and resolves problems with the integrated library system
(e.g., configuration changes, content management of the Online Public
Access Catalogue) by analyzing system information with departmental
informatics staff, contractors and vendors.</p>
<p>Analyzes client requests and assesses the resource options, choice of
information sources and retrieval result to develop and deliver
responses.</p>
<p>Analyzes and organizes data from the integrated library system to
develop and deliver reports for library management and departmental
clients. </p>
<p>Analyses and provides the appropriate service delivery relative to
operational and client needs.</p>
<p>Reviews and provides feedback on the terms of contracts; participates
in the selection process; and monitors progress of the work.  Reports
back to unit head and / or section chief.</p>
<p>Interprets and applies divisional policies and procedures; analyzes
them in order to revise or recommend changes. These policies may or may
not be clearly defined, but contribute to the audit function and/or
affect the delivery of service and access to information of departmental
employees, other government employees and the general public.</p></td>

<th><strong>EC-03</strong></th>
<td><strong>Metadata Production and Dissemination
Coordinator</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Coordinates and undertakes the preparation, editing, validation
and dissemination of statistical metadata, ensuring that it is correctly
loaded to the corporate metadata repository.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides assistance, advice and direction to internal clients
with respect to statistical metadata, documented standards and relevant
policies, directives and guidelines.</p></li>
<li><p>Recommends improvements to current or proposed methods and
procedures for producing, storing, disseminating and evaluating
statistical metadata and coordinates the testing of new procedures and
systems.</p></li>
<li><p>Writes documentation, procedures manuals and training manuals
related to the production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata, gives presentations and delivers training.</p></li>
<li><p>Assists with statistical metadata quality evaluations and with
the compilation of reports for clients and management.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in and leads working groups tasked with the
production, storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Liaises with other statistical metadata specialists, subject matter
specialists, survey managers and management in order to develop and
translate requirements into specifications for the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata and to suggest
improvements to methods, procedures and systems. Provides assistance,
advice and specialized training to subordinates and clients.</p>
<p>Writes and presents detailed documents, procedures manuals and
training manuals which explain and clarify the methods, procedures and
systems used to produce, store, disseminate and evaluate statistical
metadata and which are used by other statistical metadata specialists,
clients, and management in the maintenance and enhancement of
statistical metadata management programs.</p>
<p>Writes reports which describe statistical metadata policy
requirements and the results of metadata quality evaluations and which
are used by clients and management in the maintenance and enhancement of
statistical metadata management programs.</p>
<p><strong>4. Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of statistical metadata concepts and frameworks used within
Statistics Canada, the Government of Canada and internationally, and
knowledge of how those concepts and frameworks are used, and reflected
in, policies, methods and procedures relating to the production,
storage, dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata in order
to ensure the accurate and efficient preparation, editing and validation
of statistical metadata and to ensure that colleagues and clients
understand the importance of adhering to policies, methods and
procedures in the preparation, storage and dissemination of
metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of methods, procedures and systems used for producing,
storing, disseminating and evaluating the quality of statistical
metadata and the potential impacts of new or modified methods,
procedures and systems on workloads, outputs and clients in order to
establish priorities, timelines and training plans; to recommend
improvements to methods, procedures and training; and to explain the
methodology and results of metadata quality evaluations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of survey and statistical program subject matter concepts,
methods and techniques relevant to the collection, processing,
certification and dissemination of data in order to assess client
requirements for metadata production, storage and dissemination and to
more accurately and efficiently assess the quality of statistical
metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of software, statistical packages and programming languages
used in the production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical
metadata.</p>
<p><strong>5. Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of policies, directives and guidelines directly and
specifically related to the production, storage, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata in order to coordinate the
preparation, editing and validation of statistical metadata; to provide
assistance, advice, direction and training to clients; and to report on
metadata quality.</p>
<p>Knowledge of contacts within key client Divisions in order to
establish a liaison, evaluate program statistical metadata requirements,
provide assistance, advice and direction with respect to policy
requirements, methods, procedures, systems and training relevant to the
production, storage, dissemination and quality evaluation of statistical
metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of surveys and statistical programs within
Statistics Canada in order to locate, analyze and compare related
statistical metadata and to identify, evaluate and ensure consistency in
the methods of processing, storing, disseminating and evaluating the
quality of metadata across programs.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and of Statistics Canada policies,
directives and guidelines in order to ensure understanding and
application of the Act and these policies, directives and guidelines in
the production, storage, dissemination and quality evaluation of
statistical metadata.</p>
<p>The position must have an awareness of the Statistics and Privacy
Acts with respect to the security and confidentiality of statistical
information to ensure that actions are in compliance.</p>
<p><strong>6. Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Identifies diverse sources from which statistical metadata can be
extracted in order to improve the quantity and quality of statistical
metadata disseminated.</p>
<p>Analyses client requirements in order to determine the feasibility
of, and options for, producing, storing, disseminating and evaluating
the quality of statistical metadata consistently within and across
survey and statistical programs.</p>
<p>Reviews current production, storage, dissemination and evaluation
methods, procedures and systems to ascertain their relevancy and to
propose changes or alternative methods, procedures and systems which
would result in operational efficiencies and/or improvements in the
quality of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Analyzes changes in methods, procedures and systems used to produce,
store, disseminate and evaluate the quality of statistical metadata in
order to determine the need to update statistical metadata or adapt it
to new formats; to determine where updates are required to existing
documentation, procedures manuals and training manuals; and to determine
potential impacts on clients.</p>
<p>Analyzes and evaluates statistical metadata with respect to policy
and procedural requirements, consistency across survey and statistical
programs and other pre-defined quality measures.</p></td>
</tr>

<th><strong>EC-03</strong></th>
<td><strong>Junior Data Analyst Scientist</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Supports the conduct of studies and/or projects using
quantitative statistical data analysis methods, such as: data wrangling,
validation, mining, and visualization (in R/RStudio, Python, SQL, Java,
JavaScript or another software), as well as software development,
application of machine/deep learning and artificial intelligence
techniques for problem solving.</p></li>
<li><p>Analyses statistical data using established data management
methods.  Drafts summary analysis of qualitative and/or quantitative
data and presents findings and recommendations to colleagues and/or team
members.</p></li>
<li><p>Organizes and consolidates information relevant to the defined
objectives of studies.  Establishes and maintains work files.</p></li>
<li><p>Attends Departmental meetings, and interdepartmental and
stakeholder meetings (as required), to gather data/information and to
optimize the business value of data.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in project teams and working groups to support data
management, research and analysis, and other unit activities.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p>Judgement is required to conduct non-routine data research,
assess the relevance and reliability of the data sources and to organize
and consolidate findings.  Some judgement is required to select and
apply established methods, as appropriate.</p>
<p>The data preparation and analysis supports the work of studies and
projects and the development of reports and recommendations presented to
management by senior data analysts. The results can impact the quality
of the data analysis used by more senior analysts or project
leaders.</p>
<p>Judgement to discuss business information without divulging
confidential information.</p>
<p>Participates on project teams and working groups and provides
assistance to new employees, as required.</p>
<p>May perform administrative tasks to support the unit in the conduct
of studies, the organization and coordination of meetings and other unit
activities.</p>
<p>Personal use and care of a computer and related software; standard
office equipment, furnishings and supplies; reference material and
policy documents; and multimedia equipment. May handle sensitive
materials.</p>
<p>Uses taxi chits and travel credit cards for authorized business
purposes.</p>
<p>Conducts research and collects data from a variety of sources,
aggregating it so that it provides valid and reliable data on which to
perform quantitative, retrospective and predictive analysis that
measures the impact of the Department’s programs to support
evidence-based policy and program decisions.</p>
<p>Apply data science principles including data capture and acquisition,
data extraction, warehousing, cleansing, modeling, summarization,
reporting, visualization, and exploratory/confirmatory, predictive
analysis, regression, and qualitative analysis, to clarify the
implications of analyses for the department’s policies and programs.</p>
<p>Review data collections and information sources to determine the
usefulness of existing sources and assess the feasibility and impact of
new or modified methodologies, to identify and evaluate problems in the
processing, editing, compilation and presentation of analytical
materials.</p>
<p>Participate in research and analytics projects for the design,
development and implementation of analytic techniques, including
artificial intelligence models and data visualization to enhance
Statistics Canada’s data assets and support the evolution of analytics,
reporting processes and data governance capabilities that generate more
meaningful data in support of both operational and strategic, and
program and policy decision making.</p>
<p>Create and test data and mathematical models for bias, of particular
importance in predictive and prescriptive analytics.</p></td>

<td><strong>Operations Coordinator</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Coordinates and organizes the implementation, conduct and
maintenance of manual and automated operations for the collection,
coding and processing of data.</p></li>
<li><p>Reviews and documents operational processes and assesses the
quality and application of data outputs in order to propose solutions or
explain results.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides advice to users on the methods and procedures related to
statistical operations services.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates as a statistical operations representative on
various teams and working groups.</p></li>
<li><p>Leads the activities of work groups involved in different stages
of data collection operations.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Interprets and clarifies client requests and inquiries for
statistical data and information; responds orally or in writing to
enquiries from managers, employees and the general public to provide
information on the operation of divisional systems and processes and the
use of program results.</p>
<p>Meets with subject matter and systems specialists, clients, service
providers and others to establish and elaborate the framework and
conditions within which statistical operations will occur. There is a
requirement to understand the issues and resolve differences that can
affect the efficiency of the division's operations and quality of
outputs.</p>
<p>Contacts data suppliers or respondents to obtain their collaboration
in responding to surveys, to clarify problems or irregularities, to
explain technical procedures for obtaining the data in the appropriate
format, or to clarify and explain statistical concepts.</p>

explanatory text for use in statistical analysis by clients both inside
and outside Statistics Canada.</p>
<p>Writes specifications and guidelines to be used by others in
developing or operating systems and processes.</p>
<p>Participates in inter-departmental meetings, conferences and working
groups in order to share knowledge within an area of operational
expertise.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of the statistical and economic concepts and principles
used in the collection, coding and processing of statistical data in
order to recommend and implement improvements to survey collection,
coding and processing activities.</p>
<p>Knowledge of system analysis and design, computer processing,
generalized survey software packages to oversee the design, testing,
operation and maintenance of processing systems; to design
specifications for the testing of data, and to maintain and use
databases.</p>
<p>Knowledge of project and time management methods, practices and
techniques to develop work plans, estimate resource requirements and to
monitor and report on work progress.</p>
<p>Knowledge of methods and practices of operating personal computers in
a network environment and of using databases and software packages (word
processing, statistical analysis, graphics, spreadsheets, etc.) to
prepare correspondence, reports and presentations.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of developments and changes in the subject area that may
affect the efficiency and validity of data collection, coding and
processing in order to devise and modify operational systems and
processes.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the departmental and divisional mandate, objectives,
priorities, practices, standards, processes and procedures in order to
coordinate and manage on-going operational activities and/or special
projects.</p>
<p>Knowledge of departmental publication, marketing and dissemination
policies and practices in order to establish common divisional policies
and practices in the preparation of statistical tables, charts,
explanatory notes for dissemination to user and/or subject matter
clients.</p>
<p>Knowledge of statistical programs within Statistics Canada in order
to identify potential sources of information for operations and/or
special studies and to identify and evaluate possible data
conflicts.</p>
<p>Knowledge of contacts within key client departments to understand and
explain the need for statistical data, and to provide advice and
guidance on survey concepts, methods, techniques and results.</p>
<p>Knowledge of data sources and contacts within and external to
Statistics Canada to obtain collaboration in the provision of
statistical information and to verify the accuracy of data.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and other pertinent acts such as the
Corporate Returns Act and Income Tax Act, and of Statistics Canada
policies  to ensure understanding and application in the collection,
coding and processing of data. The position must have a particular
awareness of the Statistics and Privacy Acts with respect to the
security and confidentiality of data to ensure their own actions are
in compliance.</p>
<p>6. Research and analysis</p>
<p>Researches options and requirements for the operation of divisional
statistical systems and processes in order to assist in the
establishment of divisional and program objectives and priorities.</p>
<p>Reviews and compares the concepts and definitions of data sources
with those of the statistical program. Reconciles differences and
develops special methods of adjusting source data.  This requires
interpreting or adapting practices.</p>
<p>Develops and outlines system requirements for use by computer
analysts and programmers in revising existing systems or in developing
new processing systems.</p>
<p>Assesses trends and developments in the program area by preparing
tabulations and reviews outlining program results for use by program
managers and general data users.</p></td>

<th><strong>EC-03</strong></th>
<td><strong>Regional Geographer and Management Information Systems
Analyst</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Conducts delineation of census assignments to support Census
operations for the assigned region for all collection activities.
Identifies Field Operations Manager (FOM) boundaries. Receives,
verifies, sorts and distributes collection maps to the field.</p></li>
<li><p>Acts as the subject matter expert within the region for
geography-related issues; resolves geo-coding and boundary issues; seeks
solutions to atypical problems through consultation with the National
Geographer; and provides updates on the progress of delineation
activities at the regional and local level.</p></li>
<li><p>Receives and responds to queries from managers and staff such as
the identification of Census Field Office (CFO) or Crew Leader District
(CLD) boundaries.</p></li>
<li><p>Delivers training and/or information sessions on interpretation
of MIS reports, geography and supports collectives concepts to managers
and field staff.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in the evaluation and debriefing process of the
Census operation in respect to geography and MIS and collective dwelling
enumeration.</p></li>
<li><p>Responds to requests from RCC or CFO’s for information and
generates MIS Key Indicator Reports (KIR) and MIS detailed reports
dealing with various Census tasks including, but not limited to,
Geospatial reports, Recruiting, Pay, NRFU, List/Leave, Collectives and
Reserves.</p></li>
<li><p>Analyzes MIS reports for CFO, RCC and Census Management and
provides explanations on reporting processes as required; identifies
inconsistencies and investigates and resolves problems; advises
management and clients on the quality, reliability, nature and
availability of data; and extracts data from MIS reports and KIRs to
prepare customize reports, graphs, tables, charts using spreadsheets or
databases.</p></li>
<li><p>Accesses and utilizes the Collection Management system (CMP) and
the MIS Portal to obtain the MIS data sought by various levels of Census
management.</p></li>
<li><p>Organizes and coordinates collective dwelling data collection
activities and builds a regional database of collective organizations
(i.e. community assisted group homes, military bases and hospitals)
using historical data from previous censuses and information gathered
from web-based information systems; creates planning calendars, in-house
reports and various types of spreadsheets for collective dwelling
enumeration.</p></li>
<li></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication</strong></p>
<p>Liaises with the MIS Team (Head Office, FOP) and National Geographer,
seeks solutions to atypical situations and provides briefings on the
progress of studies/activities undertaken at the local level.</p>
<p>Writes and presents detailed subject matter and technical concepts,
definitions and terms in common terminology related to geography,
geo-coding and boundary issues in order to explain and clarify their
application.</p>
<p>Provides advice to managers and staff on matters relating to
geography and geo-coding concepts. There is a need to adapt technical
terminology for various audiences.</p>
<p>Provides training to users throughout the area on effective data
entry, retrieval and reporting techniques and practices and to managers,
colleagues and other users within the CFOs on the interpretation of MIS
reports and geographic concepts.</p>
<p>Provides assistance with MIS reports, data collection, retrieval and
reporting systems, provides instruction and guidance to authorized users
on solutions to data entry, coding and report production issues.</p>
<p>Reconciles the Collective Dwelling Listing File (CDLF) with the mail
out address file to avoid duplication and prevent incorrect enumeration
methods being applied to collective dwellings.</p>
<p>Contacts large and medium sized collective organizations such as
health care facilities, military establishments and universities in
advance of census collection activities to obtain their cooperation and
ensure their full understanding of collection procedures and Statistics
Canada’s expectations; answers any questions and resolves outstanding
issues with respect to collection procedures prior to the start of
collection; identifies contacts to assist with enumeration and obtain
building access.</p>
<p>Liaises with Regional Communications staff to obtain publicity
services and other special letters and materials required to support
