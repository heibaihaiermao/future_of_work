<th><strong>EC-02</strong></th>
<td><strong>Junior Evaluation Officer</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Participates as a member of project teams in the conduct of
evaluation studies related to programs, policies and initiatives, to
evaluate their relevance and performance, under the supervision of the
Evaluation Manager.</p></li>
<li><p>Undertakes assigned tasks related to specific elements of
evaluation projects such as, conducting literature and data research,
identifying, gathering, analyzing and synthesizing quantitative and
qualitative data; participating in the conduct of interviews, and focus
groups, applying analytical tools and techniques to determine
implications of evaluation results and formulating conclusions.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides support in the assessment of current and future
requirements for data; gathers and acquires information and analyzes
databases; and participates in the conduct of socio-economic analyses
and cost-benefit analysis of programs, under supervision.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input on the interpretation of data to support findings
and conclusions concerning various elements of evaluations. Recommends
to the Evaluation Manager, enhancements to methods for retrieving data
as well as information used in the conduct of socio-economic analyses
and cost-benefit analyses.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides the Evaluation Manager with supporting data for sections
of reports, presentations, briefing notes and memoranda for senior
management on elements of program evaluations of various
programs.</p></li>
<li><p>Attends departmental and inter-departmental meetings, when
required, to discuss files / issues and evaluation
requirements.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>Communication:</strong></p>
<p>Speaking skills are required to discuss data requirements with
Statistics Canada clients and evaluation colleagues; and to communicate
with colleagues and outside professionals to obtain data related to
Statistics Canada and external evaluation studies.</p>
<p>Speaking and writing skills are required to establish and maintain
positive relations with Statistics Canada clients and stakeholders
within and outside the Agency. The incumbent is required to communicate
with clients in the data acquisition process; prepare statistical and
narrative summaries for review by the  Evaluation Manager, provide
progress reports, and brief the Evaluation Manager on the activities and
processes being followed.</p>
<p>Verbal skills are required to participate in interviews, focus groups
and discussions with Statistics Canada clients on data requirements and
to obtain appropriate information to be used in studies.</p>
<p><strong>Knowledge of Specialized Fields:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of theories and principles of economics, socio-economic,
demography and statistics to: participate in the conduct of
Statistics Canada evaluation studies as a member of project teams, and
to interpret and analyze statistical information and databases related
to various elements of Statistics Canada evaluations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of theories, methods and techniques of research, including
social science research and analysis in order to: undertake literature
and data research in support of Statistics Canada evaluation studies;
identify, synthesize and analyze qualitative and quantitative research
data; conduct interviews and participate in focus groups.</p>
<p>Knowledge of program evaluation and performance measurement theories,
processes and practices for participating in the conduct of evaluation
studies, making recommendations concerning the nature and scope of
studies and study approaches, as well as providing supporting data for
sections of reports, memoranda and briefing notes based on elements of
studies conducted.</p>
<p><strong>Contextual Knowledge:</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge is required of the mission, mandate, organization, and
objectives of Statistics Canada accountabilities and policies. This
knowledge is required to carry out data research, collection and
compilation assignments under the supervision of the Evaluation
Manager.</p>
<p>Basic knowledge is required of mandate and evaluation policies of the
central agencies related to program evaluation, Statistics Canada, and
government priorities for Statistics Canada, to carry out data research
and compilation assignments related to Statistics Canada program
evaluation, under supervision.</p>
<p><strong>Research and Analysis:</strong></p>
<p>Undertakes literature and data research in support of
Statistics Canada evaluation studies, under the supervision of the
Evaluation Manager. The position identifies and recommends to the
Evaluation Manager the usefulness and accessible sources of data to be
used in studies.</p>
<p>Collects diverse data and information from a variety of sources,
including interviews, departmental documents and those of other
government departments, as well as information from provincial and
territorial governments and the Internet. The position also applies
established methods and techniques used for sorting, summarizing,
analyzing and interpreting data.</p>
<p>Conducts analyses and interpretation of data collected during
evaluation studies under the supervision of the Evaluation Manager, as a
junior member of project teams.</p>
<p>The objectives and purpose of the evaluation studies are defined by
the Evaluation Manager, who also determine the extent, scope and terms
of reference for the studies.</p></td>

<th><strong>EC-02</strong></th>
<td><strong>Metadata Production and Dissemination Officer</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Prepares, edits, validates and disseminates statistical metadata,
ensuring that it is correctly loaded to the corporate metadata
repository.</p></li>
<li><p>Under guidance, provides assistance and advice to internal
clients with respect to statistical metadata, documented standards and
relevant policies, directives and guidelines.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides feedback on current or proposed methods and procedures
for producing, storing, disseminating and evaluating statistical
metadata; and participates in the testing of new procedures and
systems.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input to documentation, procedures manuals and training
manuals related to the production and dissemination of statistical
metadata and assists with the delivery of training.</p></li>
<li><p>Under guidance, assists with statistical metadata quality
evaluations and provides input to reports for clients and
management.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates on teams tasked with the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p><strong>3. Communication</strong></p>
<p>Liaises with other statistical metadata specialists, subject matter
specialists, survey managers and management in order to develop and
translate requirements into specifications for the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata; to negotiate
procedural modifications, priorities and target dates; and to suggest
improvements to methods, procedures and systems.</p>
<p>Contributes written information to be used as input to documentation,
procedures manuals and training manuals which explain and clarify the
policy requirements, methods, procedures and systems used to produce,
store, disseminate and evaluate statistical metadata and assists with
the delivery of training.</p>
<p>Contributes written information to be used as input to reports which
describe the results of metadata quality evaluations and which are used
by clients and management in the enhancement of statistical metadata
management programs.</p>
<p><strong>4. Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of statistical metadata concepts and frameworks used within
Statistics Canada, the Government of Canada and internationally and
knowledge of how those concepts and frameworks are used and reflected in
policies, methods and procedures relating to the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata in order to
prepare, edit and validate statistical metadata and to provide advice
and assistance to clients.</p>
<p>Knowledge of methods, procedures and systems used for producing,
storing, disseminating and evaluating the quality of statistical
metadata and the potential impacts of new or modified methods,
procedures and systems on workloads and clients in order to provide
advice and assistance to clients; to participate in testing and to
provide feedback on current and proposed methods for producing, storing,
disseminating and evaluating metadata; to provide input to procedures
and training manuals; and to provide input to data quality reports.</p>
<p>Knowledge of survey and statistical program subject matter concepts
and methodologies relevant to the collection, processing, certification
and dissemination of data in order to assess client requirements for
metadata production, storage and dissemination and to understand the
requirements for, and instructions relating to, metadata quality
evaluations.</p>
<p>Knowledge of software used in the production, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata.</p>
<p><strong>5. Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of policies, directives and guidelines directly and
specifically related to the production, storage, dissemination and
evaluation of statistical metadata in order to prepare, edit and
validate statistical metadata; to provide assistance, advice and
training to clients; and to assist in reporting on metadata quality.</p>
<p>Knowledge of contacts within key client Divisions in order to
establish a liaison and provide assistance and advice with respect to
the production, storage, dissemination and quality evaluation of
statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of surveys and statistical programs within
Statistics Canada in order to understand program statistical metadata
requirements and ensure consistency in the methods of processing,
storing, disseminating and evaluating the quality of metadata across
programs.</p>
<p>Knowledge of program context, program sponsors, participants and
clients in order to participate in the production, storage,
dissemination and evaluation of statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the divisional mandate, activities, priorities and
practices in order to adhere to the various statistical and
administrative practices and procedures involved in the production,
storage, dissemination and quality evaluation of statistical metadata
and to provide feedback on current and proposed methods for producing,
storing, disseminating and evaluating statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and of Statistics Canada policies,
directives and guidelines in order to ensure understanding and
application of the Act and these policies, directives and guidelines in
the production, storage, dissemination and quality evaluation of
statistical metadata.</p>
<p>The position must have an awareness of the Statistics and Privacy
Acts with respect to the security and confidentiality of statistical
information to ensure that actions are in compliance.</p>
<p><strong>6. Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Compiles information from diverse sources in order to prepare, edit,
validate and evaluate statistical metadata.</p>
<p>Interprets client requirements in order to determine options for
producing, storing, disseminating and evaluating the quality of
statistical metadata and in order to determine the possible impacts of
proposed modifications in methods and procedures.</p>
<p>Analyzes current production, storage, dissemination and quality
evaluation methods, procedures and systems in order to propose
enhancements or alternatives.</p>
<p>Analyzes changes in methods, procedures and systems used to produce,
store, disseminate and evaluate the quality of statistical metadata in
order to determine the need to update statistical metadata or adapt it
to new formats and to determine where updates are required to existing
documentation, procedures manuals and training manuals.</p>
<p>Evaluates selected indicators of statistical metadata quality across
survey and statistical programs in accordance with pre-defined quality
guidelines.</p></td>

<th><strong>EC-02</strong></th>
<td><strong>Junior Data Analyst</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Supports the conduct of studies and/or projects using established
quantitative statistical data analysis methods and software such as
Python, SAS, STATA, R, E-Views, SPSS or other similar software.</p></li>
<li><p>Conducts data preparation techniques, for example in data
extraction, data cleansing, data transformation, data validation,
ascertaining data quality, conformity to standards, or data
origin.</p></li>
<li><p>Organizes and consolidates information relevant to the defined
objectives of studies. Maintains work files.</p></li>
<li><p>Attends Departmental meetings, and interdepartmental and
stakeholder meetings (as required), to gather data/information.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates in project teams and working groups to support data
management, research and analysis, and other unit activities.</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p>Communication skills are required to maintain networks across the
branch and the federal community of practice to share best practices in
advanced analytics.</p>
<p>Provides advice, information and guidance to data users and clients
regarding governance concepts used to protect and optimize the utility
of Statistics Canada’s data assets.</p>
<p>Writing skills are necessary to draft clear and simple analytical
reports, briefings and presentation material using data visualization
tools for managers and clients with varying levels of knowledge and
understanding of data science. Information must be impartial and clearly
presented to minimize the potential for misinterpretation.</p>
<p>As assigned, discusses data requirements with operations managers
responsible for data collection, other subject matter and systems
specialists, clients or service providers to comprehend and elaborate
the conditions within which statistical development and operations will
occur.</p>
<p>There is a requirement to liaise with other subject matter
specialists and users to develop and translate subject matter
requirements into specifications for data development, and to suggest
improvements to ongoing programs.</p>
<p>The work requires knowledge of data science and its life cycle,
including data capture and acquisition, data extraction, warehousing,
cleansing, processing, mining, clustering, modeling, summarization,
reporting, visualization, business intelligence, and exploratory/
confirmatory, predictive analysis, regression, text mining, and
qualitative analysis. This knowledge is required to optimize the use of
the Statistics Canada’s data assets and to provide leading edge thinking
and methodologies to data analytics.</p>
<p>The work requires knowledge of the theories, principles, practices,
and methodologies of statistics and mathematics in order to conduct the
requisite modelling that provides meaningful information for policy and
program decisions by management.</p>
<p>Knowledge of techniques and methods of information collection and
analysis, evolving information sources, survey methodologies, data risk
assessment, impact and quantitative analysis in order to assess the
quality of data and identify and mitigate risks to its integrity.</p>
<p>Data literacy skills are needed to derive meaningful information from
data, including the knowledge needed to read, analyze, interpret,
visualize and communicate data, as well as understand the use of data in
decision-making. Data literacy includes data stewardship and the ability
to assess the quality of data, protect and secure data, and safeguard
their responsible use. This is needed to develop briefings,
presentations and dashboards that contain valid and reliable data.</p>
<p>Basic knowledge of theories, principles and techniques of research is
required including; hypothesis formulation and validation; model design
and interpretation, experimental and comparative study designs; surveys,
simulations, file reviews and case studies; qualitative and quantitative
analysis including statistical sampling and cost benefit analysis;
computer assisted analysis packages; and the strengths and weaknesses of
these methods, tools and techniques in order to recommend and select
project methodologies.</p>
<p>The work requires a basic knowledge of the mandate, vision, values,
culture, organization, legislation, programs, business lines and
operations of Statistics Canada. This is needed to ensure the alignment
of the data analytics strategy with the department’s business
intelligence requirements.</p>
<p>The work requires knowledge and understanding of the governance
structures of Statistics Canada’s partnerships in order to assess data
provided by other levels of government for validity and integrity.</p>
<p>Basic knowledge of Departmental, Central Agencies and Library and
Archives Canada policies pertaining to the handling, tracking, filing
and storing of sensitive and confidential material and information
holdings, including electronic records.</p></td>
</tr>

<th><strong>EC-02</strong></th>
<td><strong>Operations Officer</strong></td>
<td><ul>
<li><p>Participates in the implementation, conduct and maintenance of
manual and automated operations for the collection, coding and
processing of data.</p></li>
<li><p>Evaluates collection and coding methods, procedures and results
and recommends alternatives.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides assistance and advice to users on methods and practices
of collection and coding.</p></li>
<li><p>Participates on data collection and operations teams.</p></li>
<li><p>Provides input to training materials concerning data collection
operations and assists in training delivery.</p></li>
<li></li>
</ul></td>
<td><p>Responds orally and in writing to enquiries from managers,
employees and the general public to provide information on the operation
of divisional systems and processes and the use of program results.</p>
<p>Writes survey information manuals, training packages, monitoring
procedures and management reports.</p>
<p>Designs and prepares statistical tables, charts, graphs and
explanatory text for use in regular and ad-hoc publications.</p>
<p>Prepares reports and recommendations outlining options and
requirements for the operation of statistical collection and processing
systems.</p>
<p>Meets with subject matter and systems specialists, clients, service
providers and others, in order to comprehend and elaborate on the
conditions within which statistical operations will occur. There is a
requirement to understand the issues and resolve differences that can
affect the efficiency of the division's operations and quality of
outputs.</p>
<p>Contacts data suppliers or respondents to obtain their collaboration
in responding to surveys, to clarify problems or irregularities. This
may necessitate having to explain technical procedures for obtaining the
data in the appropriate format, or to clarify and explain statistical
concepts.</p>
<p>Participates in departmental meetings and working groups in order to
share knowledge within an area of operational expertise.</p>
<p><strong>4. Knowledge of specialized fields</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of the underlying statistical and economic principles and
concepts required for the collection, coding and processing of data.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the concepts, methods and techniques used in the
management and control of statistical collection, coding and processing
activities.</p>
<p>Knowledge of methods, techniques and practices used to verify
statistical data and to ensure reliability and consistency to
specifications.</p>
<p>Knowledge of methods and practices of operating personal computers in
a network environment and of using databases and software packages (word
processing, statistical analysis, graphics, spreadsheets, etc.) to
prepare correspondence, reports and presentations.</p>
<p><strong>5. Contextual knowledge</strong></p>
<p>Knowledge of systems analysis and design, computer processing and
data flows, general software packages and computer languages, for
designing the specifications for testing of applications, the
application and conversion of databases, and the maintenance and use of
databases both for regular production of statistical data and for ad hoc
manipulations and retrievals.</p>
<p>Knowledge of developments and changes in the subject area that may
affect the efficiency and validity of data collection, coding and
processing in order to devise and modify operational systems and
processes.</p>
<p>Knowledge of divisional mandates, objectives, programs, policies and
activities to adhere to the various statistical and administrative
practices and procedures involved in the collection, coding and
processing of statistical data.</p>
<p>Knowledge of data sources and contacts within Statistics Canada in
order to locate, analyze and compare statistical output to ensure
consistency and reliability; and external to Statistics Canada to obtain
collaboration in the provision of statistical information and to verify
the accuracy of data.</p>
<p>Knowledge of the Statistics Act and other pertinent acts such as the
Corporate Returns Act and Income Tax Act, and of Statistics Canada
policies  to ensure understanding and application in the collection,
coding and processing of data. The position must have a particular
awareness of the Statistics and Privacy Acts with respect to the
security and confidentiality of data to ensure their own actions are in
compliance.</p>
<p><strong>6. Research and analysis</strong></p>
<p>Analyses changes to data sources and outputs, to determine their
impact on the integrity and completeness of program results.</p>
<p>Assesses and reviews system requirements for use by computer analysts
and programmers in reviewing existing systems or in developing new
processing systems.</p>
<p>Analyses reported data and work results and prepares reports for
program managers or subject matter specialists, presenting findings on
how the survey was conducted, problems encountered in implementing
concepts, definitions, classifications and other instructions, and
recommending alternatives.</p></td>
</tr>
