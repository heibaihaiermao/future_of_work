# Purpose

<span id="bookmark2">‌</span>Tier Two Committees are tasked with defining
the future state of their assigned GSBPM step in a way that is bold,
transformative, and disruptive—not just incremental improvements. The
goal is to rethink existing processes.

The vision and opportunities identified through this work will help
shape business objectives that will drive the Target Enterprise
Architecture and Business Transformation. Committees should explore
opportunities to streamline workflows, integrate emerging technologies,
and challenge traditional methods to create a future-ready statistical
system. The focus should be on ambitious changes that align with
strategic priorities.

        # Template Guide – Key Opportunities

        (in support of next slide)

        <span id="bookmark5">‌</span>The key opportunities template is
        designed to help Tier Two Committees identify and implement
        practical, high-impact changes within their assigned GSBPM step.
        The focus is on tactical execution—defining the specific
        adjustments, process modifications, and technology applications
        that will drive measurable improvements.

        Rather than broad strategic goals, this guide directs committees
        toward operational levers that can be pulled to streamline
        workflows, enhance automation, and reduce manual effort.
        Committees should prioritize actionable and scalable solutions
        that align with modernization objectives and create tangible
        benefits for both internal operations and external stakeholders.

        ## Tactics and Levers for Implementation

        #### Key Questions to Consider:

-   What fundamental shifts (levers) in our organization must change to
    achieve transformation?

-   What key business components or capabilities can be modified to
    achieve different results?

-   What tactics will be applied to accomplish these changes?

-   Are we looking to alter any key processes, frameworks, or ways of
    doing business?

-   What processes need to be modified or eliminated to drive efficiency
    and innovation?

-   How can automation and technology be leveraged to replace manual
    interventions?
